Taeyeon’s Traits

Whew— hff— whew—

Shirtless, Ji-ho racked the bar after his fifth set of squats and toweled his forehead. The system-issued squat rack was earning its keep: thighs burning, swollen, jelly-soft — the exact signature of leg endurance under construction. Sweat tracked down his chest, over the ab lines that three months of the board had carved into unmistakable relief.

Hot shower. Desk. A sip of lavender tea, honey-faint on the tongue. He closed the book at his elbow — a classic text on screen acting, lately devoured under the God of Learning’s golden hour, and worth every page — and picked up his phone.

Still no reply from Taeyeon.

Zero surprise. Given the contrarian firmware, she was currently typing and deleting, deleting and typing — desperate to talk, too proud and too shy to press send, ostrich protocol fully engaged. And if he genuinely left her hanging, the spiral would start: does he think less of me now? Was the kiss bad? Men never treasure what they’ve already— followed by solo sulking, self-consumption, possibly clandestine tears.

With this one, at this stage, you led — gently. He typed:

✦   ✦   ✦

Taeyeon, freshly showered, sat at her vanity beginning the nightly skin ritual — and the mirror ratted her out instantly: cheeks still faintly pink, lips full and soft, and when she pressed them absently together, the memory replayed itself in full tactile fidelity — cool softness first, then that dizzying warmth.

“…Terrible, terrible man…”

She mouthed it at her reflection and then grinned like an idiot at herself.

Ew. SO uncool. She slapped her cheeks twice. Composure, Taeyeon. Composure.

Essence, patted in. Her peripheral vision kept snagging on the phone beside the cotton pads. Something with small claws was scratching at the inside of her chest. Will he send something else? Will he ask why nuna’s ignoring me? Maybe a pitiful sticker—?

Cream. Eye cream. Done. She held out eleven whole seconds — then snatched the phone and lit the screen.

No new messages.

“……”

A feeling she declined to name — sixty percent deflation, forty percent injury — welled up. She opened the thread. Last message: his, about the fruit flavor. Her fingertip hovered… and typed nothing. He’s seen the read receipt by now. Good. Let him KNOW I read it and refused to answer. She savored the imagined version of his face — checked it against the ache of her own waiting — and called the result a victory.

She exited the chat with great ceremony, scrolled another app for show, locked the screen, and threw herself into bed, blanket over her head.

In the dark, the sensory recordings only played louder. His breath. The heat of him at zero distance. The last kiss on her cheek. The careful, testing softness of the first one. Strawberry sugar, shared.

“Ahh— INFURIATING!”

She kicked her legs under the blanket as if the memories could be mechanically dislodged. Her mouth curved up anyway, entirely unsupervised. Phone under the pillow. Final ruling: no reply tonight. You tormented my heart; now taste the waiting yourself.

Under the pillow: silence.

Ding-dong~

She flipped over so fast the bed complained, hand diving under the pillow. His message. OF COURSE his message.

Heehee. Couldn’t hold out, could you! That’s what you get for ambush-kissing me! I remain FATALLY attractive~

Taeyeon’s nickname among the members was well earned: her brain simply took roads other brains didn’t. A girl fresh off her first kiss should have been too shy to discuss physiques. She was not too shy. She wanted visual documentation.
