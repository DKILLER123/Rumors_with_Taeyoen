Chapter One Hundred Thirteen

Planting the Flag

One mercy: PSY’s promotions were slated to begin August 14. Which left “Some” a half-month window to collect a few more trophies — and then bow out gracefully rather than duel a comet.

August 11, 2012 MBC “Show! Music Core” — Backstage the last promotion stage · and a flag-planting ceremony

Taeyeon inspected her reflection with prosecutorial thoroughness.

Ivory lace blouse over a black high-waisted skirt; long hair in soft waves over one shoulder; diamond studs catching the vanity lights. Cool-toned face, pure and devastating at once. She pressed her lips together — strawberry balm. The flavor a certain someone had said he liked.

“Taeyeon, ready?”

Tiffany came in, pink dress setting off her fair skin — and her eyes flickered at the sight of Taeyeon’s full armament.

“You look GORGEOUS today.”

“Hosting. One should be formal.” She rose, adjusted her collar, and asked with surgical casualness, “How was Ji-ho and IU’s rehearsal?”

“Smooth.” A beat. “Their chemistry really is good.”

Taeyeon’s fingers tightened a few newtons. The smile stayed up.

“Good. Let’s go — we’re due out front.”

✦   ✦   ✦

Stage wing. IU in pale lavender, skin like first snow, makeup immaculate; Ji-ho in a simple black suit, white shirt open at the collar — severity subtracted, ease added.

“Nervous? Last promotion stage.”

“A little sad, honestly. This song gave us a lot.”

“It did.” IU smiled — and something moved underneath it. “I hear the song originally had a different story?”

He blinked — and the floor manager waved them out before he could answer.

✦   ✦   ✦

The MC interview segment: Tiffany, Seo Ju-hyun, and Taeyeon at the hosting stand. As Ji-ho and IU walked out, two of the three MCs locked onto him simultaneously.

“Welcome, Ji-ho-ssi and IU-ssi!” Tiffany, radiant. “Congratulations on ‘Some’ and its astonishing run!”

“And today is the song’s final promotion stage — how are you both feeling?”

“Truly grateful to everyone who loved this song,” IU answered. “Every time I stood on stage with ‘Some,’ I could feel it. The promotions end today — but the song stays with me.”

The microphone moved toward Ji-ho — and Taeyeon intercepted it, seamlessly.

“For a rookie’s first song to achieve all this — genuinely remarkable.” The smile was flawless; the eyes on him, warm. “But I’m curious about something. As the WRITER — when you handed ‘Some’ to IU-ssi, were you ever worried the song might not suit her? Every singer’s style is different, after all.”

A perfectly reasonable question, on paper. IU caught the stinger folded inside it instantly: the implication that the song had originally belonged somewhere else. …Have I offended Taeyeon lately?

Ji-ho’s mouth twitched once — then he fielded it, composed.

“Ne — Taeyeon nuna is exactly right. Every voice carries different colors and a different emotional grammar; the same song becomes a different creature in different hands.”

He turned to IU, tone sincere.

“IU nuna’s tone has a storyteller’s quality — clarity with a fine grain of huskiness underneath. It suits the ‘unfinished conversation’ at the heart of ‘Some’ perfectly: it puts the listener inside that regret of words that never got said, that vigil over a phone that doesn’t ring.”

“…Thank you.” IU laughed behind her hand — the analysis was startlingly professional, and being SEEN that precisely was its own pleasure.

Taeyeon’s smile held its shape. Behind it, a spark: Oh, IU SUITS it, does she. Ji-ho. You wait.

He continued, unhurried.

“In fact — other singers would make it a different song entirely.” He looked to Seo Ju-hyun. “If Seohyun nuna sang it, that clear, pure timbre might give it a first-love greenness.”

Named without warning, she pinked faintly and bowed her thanks.

He turned to Tiffany, smile going warm.

“Tiffany nuna’s tone is sweet with that unmistakable nasal resonance — a warmth like California sunlight. In her hands it would come out braver, brighter: less waiting-by-the-phone, more ‘I’m right here — walking straight up on a sunny afternoon and asking, coffee, you and me, now.’”

Tiffany’s eyes lit; the corner of her mouth escaped upward. He knows my voice THAT well? …And honestly, that IS me.

And last, his gaze settled back on Taeyeon. Their eyes met over the microphones.

“As for Taeyeon nuna…” His voice dropped a note. “Your voice has explosive emotional force — from feather-soft breath tones up through a full-power mix, an enormous range with layer after layer inside it. If nuna sang ‘Some’…”

He paused, as if hunting for the exact word. She stopped breathing without deciding to.

“…it might come out with a stronger sense of FATE. That cry at the top of your range would turn the hook into a release — the sound of two people who could have missed each other a hundred times, and were always going to end up together anyway.”

The studio went quiet for a few seconds.

Her heartbeat was a drumline in her own ears. Fate, he says. WHY is he so good at this? He praised four people — so why does it feel like the first three were scaffolding, and the last one was addressed, sealed, and delivered to ME?

“I see, I see!” Tiffany jumped in, covering the dazed silence beside her. “As expected of the man who WROTE ‘Some’ — he’s studied all our voices!”

IU had been watching in silence. The probing question. The look Taeyeon can ALMOST hide. Woman to woman — unmistakable.

“Taeyeon eonni seems especially interested in this song?” Her smile was pure sugar. “Is it a sunbae’s care for her SM hoobae?”

One frame of stiffness — then Taeyeon’s smile brightened past specification.

“Of course. Ji-ho is my dongsaeng from the same studio. What kind of seonbae doesn’t do a little promotion for her little brother?”

Seo Ju-hyun read the barometric pressure and deployed the segue.

“Then — please enjoy: Ji-ho and IU, with ‘SOME’!”

Performance

Pink-violet wash. Two figures at opposite wings, drawing together with the intro.

At the line about waking to that one person’s message, their eyes meet and the hall shrieks on cue. Second chorus: per the staging, IU reaches for his hand — and in the half-second before contact, his fingers withdraw. Half an inch. A flinch calibrated to be invisible.

She feels it. Her breath skips one beat; the yearning stage-face never moves; her hand closes firmly around his — and this time he lets it, warm palm answering.

In the MC standby area, Taeyeon stands before the monitor with her eyes riveted to two joined hands, teeth sunk into her lip.

Infuriating!

“Taeyeon — next segment.” Tiffany, gently.

One long breath in — and the flawless MC smile was back on by the time she turned.

“Understood.”

The performance ended; “Some” took the week’s first place without suspense; confetti fell on the encore as the song’s promotion run closed at full glory.

✦   ✦   ✦

Dressing room. IU pulled out her in-ears and started on her stage makeup; in the mirror, her eyes found Ji-ho.

“Taeyeon sunbae’s question today. Interesting, wasn’t it?”

His hands paused on his collar.

“MC interviews always need a talking point.”

“Mm. That’s one reading.” Her eyes were clear as glass. “Either way — a VERY interesting interview.”

The makeup artist finished and left. The room went quiet with two people in it.

Then the door swung open.

“Congratulations on first place!” Tiffany, leading; Seo Ju-hyun and Taeyeon behind.

Taeyeon’s eyes went to Ji-ho first — and she crossed the room and wrapped him in a warm, public, entirely deniable hug.

“Well done… uri Ji-ho.” Volume: one person.

“Thank you, Taeyeon nuna.”

“Thank me for what.” She straightened his collar where the hug had creased it — a small, fluent, proprietary gesture. “You were always this good.”

And in the mirror, IU watched the suspicion graduate into confirmation. The natural stance of ownership. His careful restraint — and the specific gentleness leaking through it anyway. If “Some” was really written for Taeyeon… if I sang, by pure accident, the song that was HERS — and got close to him because of it — then every move she’s made today parses perfectly. Not sunbae looking after hoobae. A woman warning off a threat.

…How interesting.

“Taeyeon eonni and Ji-ho are SO close,” IU said lightly. “No wonder he thanked you so warmly on Sketchbook.”

“SM seniors and juniors are all close.” The smile was regulation-perfect. “Like you and your producers. Right?”

Message received: “you two are colleagues. Full stop.”

“Right.” IU stood — and her sweetheart neckline dipped as she leaned, with deliberate lack of hurry, for the water bottle on the table. “Though working with Ji-ho really was a delight. He’s TOO talented.”

Straightening, she clocked the exact half-second his gaze displaced itself — and the exact degree by which Taeyeon’s eyes cooled.

“Oh, Ji-ho—” she drifted to his side, close enough that the woody edge of his cologne was legible, “—my concert next week. You’re the guest. Don’t be nervous, hm?”

“That’s—”

“Ji-ho-ya. This weekend — you haven’t forgotten we’re visiting seonsaeng-nim together?” Taeyeon, sliding in like a door closing. “You haven’t seen him ONCE since leaving SM. Careful he doesn’t expel you from the studio.”

A pretext — and a well-built one. He nodded.

“Mm, nuna. I hadn’t forgotten. I’m off this weekend.”

The LOEN dividend, on display: actual autonomy. At SM, a rookie one month post-debut with a free weekend would have been a clerical error.

IU’s smile deepened a full degree. Confirmed: she’s guarding him. From me. …I only LOOK easy to bully.

She stepped in and patted Ji-ho’s arm, feather-light.

“Aigoo — what a pity. I was going to buy you dinner this weekend. I owe you half of ‘Some’s’ success, after all.” A beat, silk-wrapped: “Oh well. Same company — we run into each other CONSTANTLY. We’ll find a time.”

Taeyeon’s face shifted through two full weather systems. Does she not UNDERSTAND the hint? …Or does she understand it perfectly, and already like him?

And that was when Tiffany — silent all this while — moved.

She walked straight to Ji-ho and, before anyone in the room could react, wound her arm through his.

“Speaking of which — SOMEONE’S gotten famous and completely stopped teaching me arrangement.” Her particular sweetness, at full concentration; the pink dress’s neckline made the arm-hug spectacularly companionable; soft warmth pressed his sleeve. “You don’t get to forget your student just because you charted.”

The air in the dressing room crystallized.

Taeyeon’s face changed. Changed again. Her nails found her palms.

Tiffany. YOU. How could— we’re best friends. He’s my BOYFRIEND—

She forced the calm down over the flame. First IU. Now Tiffany. My Ji-ho, coveted from every compass point.

IU’s eyebrow arched, entertained. Seo Ju-hyun’s eyes had gone perfectly round. And Ji-ho — feeling the warmth on his arm, smelling the familiar perfume, surveying Taeyeon’s darkening gaze, IU’s schadenfreude, Seohyun’s blank shock — thought, with genuine bewilderment:

I have exactly ONE official girlfriend. Why does this room feel like a battle royale? I haven’t DONE anything!

Tiffany looked up at him, something flickering behind her eyes.

“What — did you really forget?”

“No.” Arctic. “I’ve just decided to stop teaching.”

The hurt on her face was immediate and real.

“…Why?”

Women are baffling. YOU’RE the one who went silent — and now you look wounded?

He shrugged — the motion brushing his arm against the soft warmth wrapped around it; something odd flickered through Tiffany at the contact.

“All those lessons and not one meal bought. Not one gift. Am I your unpaid tutor? Done. Strike.”

The indignation was not performed. It was real. We talked every day, and then — nothing. Yes, fine, the timing coincided with Taeyeon and me making it official, but YOU flirted FIRST, and then just vanished. Cold-hearted woman!

Tiffany’s breath stopped. I studied with you because I wanted YOU, and you were keeping accounts on MEALS? She rolled her eyes to the ceiling.

“FINE. Fine, fine. Name the restaurant, I’ll book it.”

And WHY is she still holding the arm— Taeyeon reached over, detached Tiffany bodily, and gathered her into her own arms like repossessed property.

“I want to learn arrangement too. Make a group chat. We’ll all study together.”

“Me too—!” Seohyun, brightening.

“Hm?” Two sets of eyes — Taeyeon’s and Tiffany’s — swiveled onto the maknae with identical sub-zero calm. Seohyun converted instantly into a very quiet quail.

IU watched the whole exchange and laughed out loud. Their own front line isn’t even stable, and they’re already massing against the foreign invader.

“It seems Ji-ho is VERY popular.” She resettled into the makeup chair, eyes traveling between Taeyeon and Ji-ho. “But eonni — such devotion to a hoobae who already LEFT the company. Genuinely touching.”

“Of course I care. Ji-ho is like my little brother.”

“…’Brother.’” IU let the word hang, tail curling upward. “How fun.”

The air crackled. Seo Ju-hyun finally couldn’t bear it.

“Um — shouldn’t we go congratulate the other artists? There are still so many guests today…”

“Well said.” Bless you, Seo Ju-hyun. “I should greet the sunbaes too.” He nodded to the four of them, politeness itself. “Thank you all for today. Taeyeon nuna, Tiffany nuna, Ju-hyun nuna, IU nuna — excuse me first.”

And he was gone at a speed just short of conspicuous.

The door clicked. A short silence.

Taeyeon recovered first, smoothing her hair.

“We should get to the next waiting room.”

Passing IU’s chair, her step caught — and she said, at a volume engineered for an audience of one:

“The song — you sang it beautifully. But some things aren’t yours. And never will be.”

IU looked up, smile without a single seam.

“Whatever does sunbae-nim mean? I don’t understand. Though — one thing I WILL say: on a stage, the only thing that matters is what the audience loves. Isn’t that right?”

Four eyes met. The air between them threw sparks.

✦   ✦   ✦

Tiffany watched it all with a complicated tide moving through her.

The arm-grab — she regretted it now. Not the act. The venue. Did she love him? Probably not yet, not quite. But LIKE — undeniably: the accelerated pulse at the sight of him, the reflex tracking of his name in every feed, the pull she couldn’t kill even knowing her best friend might be — probably was—

“Let’s go, Fany.”

She inhaled and followed. At the door she glanced back once: the woman alone at the mirror was studying her own reflection, wearing a small smile with a whole campaign folded inside it.

In the corridor Taeyeon walked fast, back to her own waiting room.

“Taeyeon…” Tiffany, softly. “Are you… okay?”

“I’m fine.”

“You and Ji-ho…” A hesitation. “Are you really together?”

Taeyeon turned to face her best friend, and answered seriously.

“Fany. There are things I can’t spell out right now. But Ji-ho, to me, is… important. More important than any of you imagine.”

Tiffany looked at that grave little face — and suddenly laughed.

“That little upstart just now. SO rude.”

“As if what we have with Ji-ho is something SHE can compete with.”

“We can’t let her get smug, DaeDae. Not on our watch.”

“Thank you for backing me up in there.” Taeyeon took her hands. “We’re teammates. Sisters. Nothing changes that for me — I hope nothing changes it for you either.”

Tiffany’s smile pulled slightly at one corner.

“DaeDae. Sisters for life. Obviously.”

Taeyeon hugged her.

“Thank you.”

✦   ✦   ✦

In the van, Taeyeon pulled out her phone, found the realtor’s thread, and typed: The Gangnam apartment — I’ll take it. Please arrange the signing as soon as possible.

Then she opened Ji-ho’s chat window: Your half-day off this weekend — I’m choosing the place. Prepare for a surprise.

Send. She pocketed the phone and watched the sky dim past the glass.

Ji-eun. Try, by all means. Ji-ho is mine. He’s been mine since that one night in Jeonju — and that was decided long before you ever sang a note of my song.

✦   ✦   ✦

Parking garage. Ji-ho in the back seat; IU at the window, chin on her hand; two managers up front.

He replayed the dressing room: Taeyeon’s flag-planting embrace. IU’s silk-wrapped provocations. Tiffany’s ambush arm.

Possessiveness. Competitiveness. Confusion. One room.

The corner of his mouth rose a millimeter. Being wanted by extraordinary people, worried over, fought across… as sensations go — not bad.

The phone buzzed. Taeyeon: a surprise. Anticipation arrived on schedule. A woman with a crisis alarm is a proactive woman.

Nuna — I have a surprise for you too.

Send. Another message dropped in on its heels — Tiffany: Sorry about today. I was impulsive. …We can talk music more, later.

He looked at the screen for a while, and in the end replied to nothing.

Nuna — the truth is I’m a bad man. I’m just still missing a few pieces of the empire.

He leaned back and closed his eyes. “Some” was done: ten first-place trophies, a full All-Kill, the year’s Triple Crown — a debut that qualified as a miracle by any measure. Undone only by a comet.

Ending promotions early costs something. But you don’t fight “Gangnam Style” for silver. Better to go quiet, go deep — and build the first album.

Not a good album. A LEGENDARY one.
