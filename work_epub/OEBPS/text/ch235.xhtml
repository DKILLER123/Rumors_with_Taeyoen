Chapter Two Hundred Thirty-Five

The Birthday Present

August 5, 2013 · night Grand Walkerhill · the Moonlight wing candles, a cream war, one photograph for the ages — and an envelope on a pillow

“Saengil chukha-hamnida~”

“Saengil chukha~ hamnida~”

“Hooo~~”

Tiffany pressed her palms together, made her wish, and blew the candles out in one long breath. In the instant the little flames died, her curved smile-eyes were two bright crescents floating in the dark.

“Woohoo~!”

Applause, laughter, candle-smoke — the room glowing pink at its edges.

“Chairman Song. Photo duty.”

Yoon-a’s porcelain hand delivered her phone. He took it and backed up several paces.

“Ya, ya — picture time, picture time.” She clapped twice. “Ppalli ppalli!”

The girls surged into formation — which is to say, chaos. Girls’ Generation was long past the era of jockeying for photo position; at this altitude, the joy was in sabotage.

Seohyun wedged herself forward, both scissor-hands deployed.

“AH!” Yuri bared her teeth. “Ya — that’s my FOOT!”

Taeyeon’s small hand snuck out and executed a precision strike on Yoon-a’s backside.

“Yoon-a! Shift your butt over!”

“YA! Taeyeon eonni, hands OFF my butt!” — trapped mid-formation, unable even to turn around.

Sunny body-checked Soo-young’s shoulder.

“Ya, Soo-young-ah, crouch a little. Congratulations on the legs, now BEND them.”

Soo-young rolled her eyes to the ceiling and obediently dipped at the knees.

Beside Taeyeon, Jessica rose briefly onto tiptoe, comparing crowns, and her soft voice arrived scandalized:

“Ani— is Taeyeon TALLER than me now?”

Push, shove, regroup — and finally nine girls assembled around the birthday girl, who was squeezed in the middle laughing until her eyes vanished entirely.

“One, two, three — forever—”

“GIRLS’ GENERATION!”

Click. Yoon-a’s phone froze the moment forever.

Forever Girls’ Generation, he thought, looking at the screen — nine faces mid-laugh, blazing. In the history he remembered, next year’s birthday would have been the last one all nine spent together. His eyes lifted to Jessica, still arguing height with Taeyeon. Not this time. This time he was here — and THAT particular heartbreak was simply not going to be permitted. Taeyeon would never have to live that day.

Jessica, come to think of it, had proven steadier than expected. Since the dinner where he’d dismantled her investment pitch in six points, she hadn’t reached out once — world tour, probably, or pride healing. The better news: with him occupying this timeline, she had never met the man who’d once rearranged her whole future. The shoe-horn-faced one. Good riddance to an unmade acquaintance.

And then Jessica glanced up mid-argument, caught the chairman studying her with an expression of unaccountable depth, and felt a chill spider down her spine. WHY is he looking at me like that?

“AAAAAAH!!!!”

Tiffany’s shriek split the garden. He and Jessica snapped around in unison.

The birthday girl had become a small calico cat — face frosted white, cream in her lashes — while Taeyeon stood over her with a fistful of cake and laughter folding her in half.

“KIM TAEYEON!!”

Tiffany swiped her face clear, scooped a counter-charge of cream, and delivered it directly onto Taeyeon’s milk-soft cheek.

The laughter cut off like a switch. Now it was everyone ELSE’S turn to laugh.

“Ya. Tiffany. You are DEAD.”

Taeyeon armed herself and fired. Tiffany ducked — and the cream sailed past her into the spectating Yuri, blooming white across healthy tan.

“YA! KIM TAEYEON!”

Yuri rolled her sleeves and entered the war.

The garden detonated. Cream in arcs. Screaming. Someone’s shirt dragged sideways off a shoulder; someone’s hem flagged up over a knee; summer clothes and total abandon everywhere. WHO THREW THAT AT MY— AISSHI— AHAHAHAHA. He prudently relocated his gaze to the horizon. Then relocated it back, briefly. Twice.

Yoon-a surveyed the battlefield — Taeyeon and Tiffany locked in single combat, ignoring all other belligerents — sighed like a diplomat, and then spotted the one dry man at the party spectating from cover. Her eyes performed a slow, wicked rotation. She loaded one palm with cream.

“Ya — LADIES! Ji-ho is over there ENJOYING the show!”

The duel stopped mid-swing. Two cream-frosted faces turned as one.

They looked at each other. They smiled — identically, coldly, in perfect sync — and advanced on him in formation. THE SCOUNDREL. GET THE SCOUNDREL.

He watched them come and made the professional decision not to run. Instead he fixed his gaze on the instigator. Yoon-a’s scalp prickled under it; the cream in her hand hovered between deployment and retreat.

“Ya! What are you looking at ME for!”

Taeyeon and Tiffany closed in from both flanks — cream-streaked, expressions ferocious, and, atop those two milk-smeared faces, about as menacing as a pair of static-charged kittens.

“Ji-ho-ssi~” Taeyeon dragged the syllables like a blade over a whetstone. “Enjoying the VIEW?”

Tiffany skipped negotiation entirely and charged with cake.

He didn’t dodge. He put one gentle palm on top of her head and held her at precisely arm’s length.

“What did you wish for?”

“If I say it, it won’t come tr— YA! You’re changing the subject!!”

One heartbeat of distraction was all he needed.

He slipped sideways past both of them — and went straight for Yoon-a.

“Ya! YA YA YA—!”

She spun to flee; his stride ate the distance in two steps. Cornered, she went for broke and slung her cream at his face. He tilted his head; it whistled past his ear —

— and scored a direct hit on Sunny, who had been enjoying the spectacle harder than anyone present.

Sunny’s laugh stopped. She looked down at the cream. She looked up.

“Im. Yoon. A.”

“MIANHAE! It was meant for HIM! He DODGED!”

Sunny was past the age of explanations. She armed both hands and came on. Yoon-a shrieked and dove for cover — behind Ji-ho — and he rotated smoothly, caught her wrist, and reeled her back out of cover, presenting her to the incoming barrage like a shield with excellent hair.

“??? Ji-ho, you are NOT HUMAN—”

The cream arrived.

Her pale face iced white, eyes enormous, lashes frosted — equal parts pitiful and hilarious. One second of total silence. Then the garden went up in the loudest laugh of the night: Taeyeon slapping her own knee, Tiffany crouched on the ground wheezing, even Jessica’s glacial composure cracking at one corner. Soo-young pointed with her whole arm: “YA! Even YOU get a turn someday! HAHAHA!”

Yoon-a stamped her foot hard enough to dent the lawn — then turned on Ji-ho with slitted eyes and a slow, dangerous smile, and began reaching, with theatrical leisure, toward the cake table.

“Yoon-a-yah.” He adopted the tone of a hostage negotiator. “Let’s be calm. Let’s discuss this.”

“Oh, absolutely~ Come closer. We’ll discuss it in DETAIL.”

“Do I look stupid to you?”

“You look DELICIOUS. Hold still.”

She charged. He ran. They orbited the party twice at speed —

“YA! Stop RUNNING!”

“Stop and get frosted? Pass.”

“STAND STILL!”

“Drop the cream and I’ll stand still.”

“IN YOUR DREAMS!”

— and then he braked without warning and turned. She had no time to arrest; she slammed straight into his chest. He relieved her of the cream in one motion and, with the other hand, repainted her face a second coat.

Yoon-a: …

“Symmetrical now.” He inspected the work and nodded. “Better.”

She inhaled. Inhaled again. And then — she smiled. Gently. SWEETLY. His stomach dropped a floor.

“Girls~” The call sailed across the garden like a hunting horn. “Someone’s been bullying your Yoon-a~”

And they came — Taeyeon, Tiffany, Sunny, Yuri, even Soo-young, converging from every compass point with cream on their hands and justice in their eyes.

“I— ladies— I can explain—”

“No explanation needed.” Yoon-a’s smile had achieved supervillain purity. “Girls — TAKE HIM.”

He vanished under a tide of cream and laughter.

✦ ✦ ✦

Five minutes later, Ji-ho sat frosted like a wedding cake, wearing the expression of a man contemplating the sea.

Around him, Girls’ Generation had collapsed into one laughing heap. Yoon-a laughed hardest of all, eyes bent to crescents, all trace of her own two-coat humiliation forgotten. Tiffany circled the wreckage with her phone: “Archiving! This is going in the PERMANENT archive!”

He wiped a slow stripe of cream from his face and regarded Yoon-a from under it.

“Yoon-a. I’m writing this down.”

“Llllle~” She stuck out her tongue. “Write it in cursive.”

“Aigoo — our great Chairman Song wouldn’t be PETTY, surely?” Taeyeon, from the sidelines, deeply unhelpful.

He looked at her for one measured second.

“Taeyeon. You’re not exempt either.”

“Ya! I’m your NUNA—”

The sentence died as he reached out and deposited the last of his cream, delicately, on the tip of her nose.

Silence. Then: “SONG JI-HO!!!” — and the war’s second act began.

It burned itself out eventually — everyone winded, the cake reduced to archaeology, nobody minding in the least. Yuri looked around at the cream-plastered wreckage of nine national treasures and started giggling for no reason at all. “It’s good,” she said, when Taeyeon looked over. And didn’t explain, and didn’t need to. Time with these people. That was the whole sentence.

He leaned against the sofa leg and watched them — loud, undignified, luminous. GOOD, he thought, in exactly Yuri’s grammar. His eyes found Taeyeon’s across the garden at the precise moment hers found his, and they looked at each other’s ridiculous frosted faces, and both laughed at once.

Yoon-a had migrated to his elbow at some point, unannounced.

“Hey.”

He looked at her.

“Look.” She pointed at her own cheek, where cream still clung. “Your work.”

“Deserved.”

She glared — and then laughed anyway, and thought, not for the first time: if only he weren’t a wandering-hearted scoundrel. Taeyeon eonni happy, and the rest of them gaining one powerful, talented brother-in-law — the arithmetic would be so clean. …Lee Seung-gi had been circling lately, asking her to dinner. Popular, well-reviewed, well-connected. A perfectly good stream. But she had already been moved by the SEA — the Alps, the man carrying her down the mountain on his back, her heart going strange — and who stops for a stream after the sea? She’d decline the dinner. And if certain rumors about enlistment-timing publicity stunts were even half-true — decline it TWICE.

She reached out and poked his arm.

“Thank you, by the way.”

“For what?”

“…Don’t know. Just felt like saying it.”

Women, he concluded, remained the one system no card in his inventory could parse.

“Ya, Yoon-a — post the photos in the group chat. I need one for Instagram.” Soo-young, from across the garden.

“Arasseo.”

“Soo-young-ah, eat first. Look at this table — it’s all obviously expensive. Photos post whenever.”

The nation’s foremost food deity considered Yuri’s argument on its merits and found it unimpeachable. And while Soo-young’s attention was thus honorably occupied, Yuri quietly posted her own photo first. Hehe. All the fan comments would land on HER account tonight. Within a minute the replies were rolling in: EONNI, HAPPY SIXTH ANNIVERSARY!! · Knew it — Fany’s birthday + debut day = full assembly! · DAEBAK, the garden behind you is gorgeous— And within two minutes, Soo-young looked up from her plate, took in Yuri’s telltale phone-grin, and processed six years of pattern recognition instantly: “YA! KWON YURI! You posted FIRST again, didn’t you!” “Ani?? Never.” “LYING! You’re DEAD!”

Through all of it, Taeyeon sat with her chin in her hand, smiling her small foolish smile at the noise. To her, this had never been merely a job. She believed the words the way the fans believed them — FOREVER, GIRLS’ GENERATION — believed that every girl in this cream-ruined garden believed them too. The sisterhood, yes. And the empire they’d built together. Both.

✦ ✦ ✦

They finished dinner in their frosting. Eventually he rose.

“There are six rooms — two with heated soaking tubs. Taeyeon nuna and I take one; the birthday girl gets the other. The rest of you, divide the four as you like.”

“Sleepwear and swimsuits are in the rooms. Rest inside, or swim in the garden pool — it’s all private. Clothes go in the laundry cabinets at the corridor’s end; staff will have them cleaned and returned by morning. And relax — I’ll be in the room with Taeyeon nuna. The garden’s all yours.”

“Understood~ We won’t disturb the sweet time. Only—” Soo-young’s eyebrows performed a full gymnastics routine. “—do try not to BREAK our leader.”

“YA! What are you SAYING!” Taeyeon combusted instantly, scarlet to the ears.

“Soo-young eonni!!” Seohyun, equally red, on principle.

Sunny and Yoon-a looked at Tiffany — placid, smiling, unreadable — then at each other, and looked away. Yuri and Hyo-yeon piled onto the heckle; Yuri was expansive: “Taeyeon-ah, what’s there to be shy about? A capable boyfriend is a BLESSING—” “I’m so jealous honestly, mine was—” “MMPH—” Seohyun got a hand over the offending mouth before the sentence could finish, very nearly in tears. “Eonni. Please do not say EVERYTHING.”

Taeyeon looked at Tiffany. Tiffany looked back — level, sweet, giving nothing. And something in Taeyeon simply… stood up straight.

She grabbed Ji-ho and kissed him. Thoroughly. In front of all eight sisters.

“OHHHH!”

“Wahhh—”

“AAAAH!”

“Ya — WHO said I was shy?” She kept one arm around him, chin high, empress of the garden. “Sweetheart. We’re going to our room.”

“THAT’S our leader!” Soo-young, thumb raised.

“Taeyeon-ah! FIGHTING!”

Tiffany watched the two of them go, her smile perfectly maintained. Yoon-a, watching her profile, sighed somewhere private. One side of the scale: love. The other: sisterhood. Eonni — right now, inside there — what are you thinking?

“I’m going to go wash up.”

Tiffany rose and walked to her room alone.

The door clicked. She leaned back against it and pressed both hands over her face. Tiffany. It’s fine. It’s FINE. You knew the shape of this the day you decided to accept him. This is the price as agreed. Breathe.

She crossed toward the bed — and stopped.

On the covers: folded sleepwear. And beside it, a thick document envelope, and a card. She picked up the card — his handwriting, unhurried and sure:

Card · left on the pillow — his handwriting

Nuna — saranghae. The envelope is your birthday present. The passcode is your birthday.

She set down the card and lifted the envelope — heavier than paper had any right to be. Inside: a property contract.

A villa. Five billion won.
