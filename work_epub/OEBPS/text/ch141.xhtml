Chapter One Hundred Forty-One

The First Sign

November 2012 · Night Banpo, Seoul — Acro River Park the evening after the tour finale

The tour was over, but IU’s work wasn’t — closing interviews, sign-offs, a staff dinner she couldn’t skip. So Ji-ho left first with his manager, and somewhere along the ride, a short exchange of messages settled the night’s itinerary: her place. Tonight.

His manager dropped him at his own building. He waited for the taillights to disappear around the corner, brought his own car up from the garage, and drove the familiar direction with the ease of water finding its channel.

He’d been inside Taeyeon’s apartment for barely five minutes when the keypad at the door sang its soft electronic chirp.

The door opened on Taeyeon still in her stage outfit from the evening’s music show, makeup camera-fresh, lethal at close range.

He reached out and gathered her straight into his arms. She got out half a startled breath before his mouth closed over hers and kept it.

Click. The door swung shut on its own weight.

They stood tangled together in the entryway, and the kiss went long and deep and slightly desperate at the edges — as if every day they’d spent apart had filed a claim, and all of them were being settled at once. He lifted her by the waist, set her back against the wall, and his hand slid beneath the hem of her top onto skin like warmed silk—

The doorbell rang.

Ding-dong. Ding-dong.

She surfaced like a swimmer who’d forgotten which way was up, shoving at his shoulder in a panic.

“Someone’s HERE—” Her breathing was wrecked; her lips were wet and bright.

A crease of honest displeasure crossed his brow at the interruption. He let go anyway.

She went up on her toes at the peephole, took exactly one look, and her whole face changed.

“It’s Yoon-a and Sunny!” she hissed over her shoulder, hands flying through a frantic pantomime of hide — hide — HIDE, eyes wide with catastrophe.

Then she smoothed the collar he’d crumpled, raked her fingers through her hair, and took several deep breaths to chase the flush off her cheeks before she finally turned the handle.

On the other side, Yoon-a and Sunny stood wearing two of the sunniest smiles in the industry.

Both smiles paused. Two pairs of eyebrows rose in perfect unison.

“Eonni… why is your face so red?” Yoon-a blinked her clear doe eyes, tone innocent as morning. “Is the heating up too high in there?”

“N-no— I was just… doing a bit of exercise.” She stepped aside to let them in, working hard to keep her voice level.

Sunny crossed the threshold first, and her gaze drifted — purely by chance, of course — toward the shoe cabinet.

Where a pair of men’s sneakers, conspicuously oversized, lay in quiet repose.

The corner of her mouth curved by a millimeter. She said nothing at all.

“So what brings you two here out of nowhere?” The door shut; her heart was hammering; she reached for the conversation’s steering wheel with both hands.

“The milk, obviously.” Sunny could barely wait to say it. “Didn’t you promise to find out the secret to getting taller? I couldn’t hold out until tomorrow. Came to collect the teachings in person.”

Even as she said it, her eyes were making a leisurely, deeply interested circuit of the living room.

“I missed you, eonni.” Yoon-a beamed, sat herself down on the sofa with the ease of family, and scooped a cushion into her arms.

Her gaze rested lightly on the men’s shoes — then lifted to Taeyeon’s slightly-too-composed face.

“About the milk— let me ask properly tomorrow, then I’ll tell you everything.”

Taeyeon retreated to the island of the open kitchen and busied herself pouring water no one had requested.

“Don’t wait for tomorrow.” Sunny settled comfortably into the sofa and regarded her with the calm of a chess player who has already seen the mate in three. “Just have Ji-ho come out. We saw the shoes.”

Taeyeon’s hand jumped; the water nearly went over the rim.

“Wh-what Ji-ho? I don’t know any—”

“Taeyeon-ah.” Sunny sighed with the weariness of a critic at a very bad play. “I live under the same roof as you. I am your ROOMMATE.”

“Lately your eyes shine. You smile at nothing. You hug your phone and answer messages with your whole soul — I know that look better than my own face. Add the height, add the glow — what else could it be but dating? And the only person you’d hide THIS carefully… the one you’ve been circling for months, the one you already had a scandal with… I honestly can’t produce a second candidate.”

Yoon-a lent her support, raising her voice helpfully toward the apartment at large:

“Ji-ho, stop hiding. Come on out. Isn’t it exhausting in there?”

Taeyeon bit her lower lip, cheeks burning hotter, caught with no exits — and then the master bedroom door opened with a soft click.

He walked out and offered the two uninvited guests a small, impeccable smile.

“Sunny nuna. Yoon-a nuna. Good evening. What a coincidence.”

And Taeyeon — the game being well and truly up — discovered that what she actually felt was relief.

She crossed quickly to his side, slipped her hand around his arm, and said it quietly, clearly, without a single wobble:

“So— formal introductions. This is my boyfriend. Ji-ho.”

“Wahh—!!”

Two voices at once, theatrically scandalized, both faces stamped with I KNEW it and caught red-handed at last in equal measure.

“You buried it DEEP, Taeyeon!” Sunny’s eyes glittered with wicked delight. “All those months swearing he was just a dongsaeng you happened to be close to?”

“Exactly, exactly.” Yoon-a — who had known since the Alps and was now giving a flawless performance of finding out this very minute — let her gaze swing between the two of them, smiling. “No WONDER your skin’s been glowing lately. It was love watering the garden all along!”

Then Sunny appeared to remember something, and her grin turned deeply archaeological.

“Omo. Hold on. Didn’t SOMEBODY once declare he’d consider marrying ME for the SM shares? Well — look at that. The acquisition roadmap is coming along beautifully, isn’t it?”

Her eyes traveled with great significance from Ji-ho to Taeyeon and back.

“Nuna. A joke. It was a JOKE.” Both hands went up in surrender.

Taeyeon, remembering the same conversation, went scarlet to the ears, shot Sunny a reproachful glare — and quietly tightened her grip on his arm.

✦   ✦   ✦

With the teasing quota satisfied, Sunny returned to what she plainly considered the real agenda, eyes going bright with mission focus.

“So. The milk. What brand is it, exactly?”

“Yonsei Milk.” Not one flicker; the name arrived smooth as a practiced signature.

A small Korean label famous for quality and priced to match — obscure enough to be plausible, premium enough to explain anything.

“Although — Sunny nuna,” he added, tone earnest to a fault, “honestly, I think Taeyeon nuna growing taller is mostly her own constitution. Or she caught the very tail of a second growth phase. It may not really be the milk at all.”

“I know. I KNOW.” She waved this off, faith entirely undented. “But if there’s a one-in-ten-thousand chance, I’m taking it. I start drinking tomorrow!”

From there the talk drifted naturally to the new album. He confirmed what the schedule already said: release set for the first week of November, everything in motion.

“I’ll help you promote it.” Taeyeon said it instantly — and then looked at Yoon-a and Sunny. Pointedly. Radiantly. In a manner impossible to misread.

The two of them exchanged one glance of total mutual comprehension.

Their leader was openly canvassing votes for her boyfriend. And once the leader has spoken — what kind of in-laws withhold support?

“We’ll recommend it on SNS too.” Nearly in unison.

Taeyeon nodded, satisfied, mouth curving upward — every inch the head of the household accepting tribute.

The chatter ran on; night thickened against the windows. Then Yoon-a stretched her arms overhead and made an announcement.

“Aigoo, it’s so late. Going home is SUCH a hassle. Taeyeon eonni — we’re sleeping here tonight, yes? I’ve never once stayed over at your place!”

“Seconded! I’m staying too!” Both of Sunny’s hands shot into the air.

The smile on Ji-ho’s face stiffened by a degree measurable only in a laboratory.

The long-awaited reunion night — the one absence was supposed to make sweeter than a honeymoon — was capsizing in real time, in front of him, with witnesses.

“You’re… actually staying?” It escaped before he could file it for review.

Yoon-a immediately wrapped both arms around Taeyeon like a mother animal shielding her young, leveling her beautiful eyes at him with maximum theatrical menace.

“Ya, Ji-ho. Understand the situation clearly. Between us and her — YOU are the latecomer.”

Her tone turned solemn as a vow.

“We are Girls’ Generation now, Girls’ Generation later, Girls’ Generation FOREVER!”

Taeyeon burst out laughing and hugged Yoon-a right back, visibly luxuriating in the reinforcements.

He could only shake his head and smile, a man watching his evening sail away on a ship he wasn’t allowed to board.

Three women unanimously exiled him to the bathroom to wash up — with an explicit warning against eavesdropping on girls’ private talk. He collected a change of clothes and went quietly, escorted out of his own night.

✦   ✦   ✦

The bathroom door closed. The living room’s decorum dropped by half instantly.

“So-o-o. Happy now, our Taeyeon?” Sunny leaned in, grin fully criminal.

Taeyeon touched the tip of her nose, which had gone warm, and her mouth curved with helpless honesty.

“He’s… very good to me.”

“Aigoo, who asked about THAT.” Sunny dropped her voice, eyebrows semaphoring. “I mean the OTHER kind of good. Look at the nose on him. Look at the training. Look at how much authority there is in the way he moves — tsk, tsk. And don’t think I haven’t clocked our Taeyeon coming home from her little ‘nights out,’ all worn-thin and glowing at the same time. I see EVERYTHING.”

“LEE SOON-KYU!!” Taeyeon’s whole face ignited; she lunged to cover Sunny’s mouth — but the light dancing in her eyes and the conspicuous absence of any denial had signed the confession already.

He— he really is… incredible.

Even Yoon-a couldn’t hold out. She leaned in, voice gone tiny.

“Eonni. What is it… actually like?”

Taeyeon, defenses exhausted, abandoned language entirely and launched herself at her.

It went poorly. Yoon-a was strong — appallingly strong — and pinned her to the sofa inside two seconds, at which point Taeyeon abandoned honor as well and resorted to guerrilla tactics no member of a national girl group would dignify with public comment. Sunny, spectating happily from a cushion’s distance, was dragged in within the minute. Three grown professionals dissolved into a shrieking sofa scrum, collars askew, dignity in full retreat.

They collapsed at last in a breathless heap.

At exactly that moment, the shower shut off.

Three women snapped upright at synchronized speed, restored their clothing to order, and commenced a solemn panel discussion of tomorrow’s weather.

He emerged toweling his hair, changed into loose sleepwear whose open collar had opinions of its own, clean lines of muscle appearing and vanishing as he moved. He nodded politely at the weather panel and went straight into the master bedroom.

The door clicked. Sunny’s elbow found Yoon-a’s ribs instantly.

“Ya! Did you SEE that? Those lines — those thighs— daebak!”

“Taeyeon eonni truly is… blessed,” Yoon-a murmured, nodding with a connoisseur’s gravity.

Taeyeon, physically unable to remain in the conversation, invented an urgent need to prepare the guest-room bedding and fled — pursued by two entirely unrestrained bursts of laughter.

✦   ✦   ✦

When the laughing was done, Sunny got up to help with the guest bed.

Yoon-a got up too — and walked to the master bedroom door, and knocked, very softly.

He opened it, mildly surprised, and stepped aside to let her in. The door stayed ajar behind her. Noted — by both of them.

Before she could speak, he struck first.

“Yoon-a nuna. Relax. I never told Taeyeon nuna about you liking me.”

She nearly choked on her own breath. Her eyes went enormous — mortified and furious inside a single expression.

“YA! I told you that was a misunderstanding! A MIS-UN-DER-STAND-ING! Ji-ho, how did I never notice what a narcissist you are?”

He leaned back against the wall, arms crossed, and assumed an attitude of mock self-appraisal.

“Am I so deficient? Not worth anyone’s liking?”

She opened her mouth — and nothing came out. Her gaze had turned traitor, wandering over him without authorization: the clean scent of the shower still on him, black hair damp, and that entirely ordinary sleepwear failing entirely to conceal the wide shoulders, the narrow waist, the suggestion of the chest beneath—

It’s been— what, a few weeks? How is he BETTER-looking?

And… is he taller? He wasn’t this looming last time. I would remember the looming.

She had to admit it — privately, under oath to absolutely no one: the Ji-ho in front of her outshone every version of him in her memory, radiating a pull that bordered on the aggressive. Probably THAT was the trick. Probably the magnetism was manufacturing the extra centimeters and the extra handsomeness out of thin air. Probably.

Out loud, surrender was unthinkable. Her chin went up.

“Not deficient, FINE — but nowhere near ‘everyone likes you’ either! Stop admiring yourself!”

And with one small hmph she turned, pulled the door open, and was gone — the backs of her ears flaming red.

The master bedroom door swung gently closed again.

✦   ✦   ✦

Back in the guest room the bed was made, and Taeyeon and Sunny were propped shoulder to shoulder against the headboard, mid-conversation. Yoon-a climbed up and settled in against Taeyeon’s side.

“Eonni, your place is HUGE,” she sighed at the ceiling.

Sunny rolled onto her side, curious.

“When you bought this apartment… it was partly for him, wasn’t it?”

No denial. Her voice simply went soft.

“A very large part of the reason… was him.”

Yoon-a propped herself up on an elbow, fully invested now.

“Eonni, tell us how it happened! From the beginning. Who chased whom?”

“Yes, YES—” Sunny scrambled closer. “Who moved first? Who kissed whom first? Talk! TALK!”

“He chased me. All of it — he started it.”

And she told it, warming as she went. The singing lessons where he’d played worse than he was, just to keep her coming back. The production room during “Some,” where the distance between two chairs kept mysteriously shrinking. The candy pressed into her palm. The first kiss that arrived out of a clear sky. The production room again, when neither of them could stop — and then the day the company found out, and the summons that followed.

She told them how he’d stepped in front of her. How he’d faced Kim Young-min and Lee Soo-man alone and taken the whole weight of it onto his own shoulders.

How, in thirty minutes, he’d written a song the two most powerful men in the building could not refuse — and then laid a wager on the table audacious enough to turn an execution into a negotiation.

“After that… he left SM. The company doesn’t interfere with us anymore. We only have to keep it quiet for a while.”

She finished it gently, the way you set down a full cup.

Yoon-a and Sunny had gone completely still. Only now did they understand what had actually been buried under the headlines — a story with that much nerve in it, and that much cost.

And only now did a recent small mystery quietly solve itself: why their leader had been faintly, deniably prickly about IU. “Some” was practically the song the two of them had fallen in love inside. Anyone’s heart would snag on that.

Their private appraisal of Ji-ho, meanwhile, climbed several floors without either of them saying so out loud. Whatever else was true — the man stood in front. In an industry where a woman’s dating scandal always costs her more, he had not retreated one step. That posture alone could move a heart.

“In that moment… eonni must have felt so safe,” Yoon-a said softly.

Handsome. Responsible. Talented. Steady-tempered. The longer she itemized the list, the clearer the envy at the bottom of her chest became — and somewhere in there, without ceremony, her standards for a future boyfriend revised themselves upward. Considerably.

Sunny, naturally, required the next chapter: how exactly had the final line been crossed?

A shy flush climbed Taeyeon’s face.

“Last time I went home to Jeonju… he followed me down. He’d written me a song. He took me camping. And then—”

She did not finish the sentence. She did not have to. The two of them traded a look of complete understanding: Ji-ho, you operator. The DEPTH of the playbook.

Had the accused been present, the defense would have objected strenuously: SHE invited HIM to Jeonju. SHE told him to plan the itinerary. And the lingerie she happened to be wearing that night had not coordinated itself by accident. Who exactly schemed against whom remains, in this court’s opinion, very much an open question.

✦   ✦   ✦

The night deepened; drowsiness drifted down like snow.

Taeyeon — too embarrassed to walk to the master bedroom in front of her own members — announced she would sleep in the guest room. Sunny and Yoon-a evicted her with the wordless coordination of long practice.

“Enough, enough. We are not villains — we don’t actually split up mandarin ducks.” Sunny flapped a hand at her. “Go on. We can’t ruin your reunion night for real.”

Only — just before the door closed, Sunny’s head reappeared through the gap, voice lowered to a stage whisper of maximum mischief.

“Taeyeon-ah. This apartment of yours. How’s the soundproofing? Because if it’s not great— pace yourself, hm?”

“YA! LEE SOON-KYU!”

Her entire face on fire, Taeyeon fled down the hall.

In the master bedroom he was propped against the headboard with his phone. The moment she appeared he put it away and opened both arms.

“Nuna. Come here.”

She climbed obediently onto the bed and burrowed into his chest — then hesitated.

“Ji-ho-ya. Tonight… let’s just sleep. Actually sleep. Okay?”

“Nuna, isn’t that still a week away?” Genuine puzzlement, and zero tact.

“Yoon-a and Sunny are right across the hall. I… I definitely won’t manage to stay quiet.”

He had an armful of warm, impossible curves, and every cell of him voted against the motion on the floor. He leaned down and kissed her cheek instead, hands tracing geography they had long since memorized.

“Mm-hm.”

She never stood a chance. He smelled too good — and he knew her body far, far too well.

They rolled together into the duvet, and out of the churning white landscape one article of clothing after another went sailing into the lamplight.

✦   ✦   ✦

The guest room was quiet. Slightly too quiet.

The building’s soundproofing was, in fact, excellent, and Taeyeon was, in fact, trying extremely hard. There was nothing strange to hear.

The atmosphere was strange anyway.

The girls of once-upon-a-time were growing up, one by one. Girls’ Generation was drifting — slowly, and for real this time — into dating’s generation.

Sunny rolled over and studied the shape of Yoon-a in the dark.

“Yoon-a. Why did you stay tonight?”

A long pause. When the answer came, it sounded genuinely lost.

“I… I don’t know either.”

Silence for a while. Then—

“Eonni. Don’t you think… Ji-ho’s gotten better-looking?”

“You feel it too?” Sunny sat up. “It’s the same face. The exact same face. And somehow it’s MORE.”

“You think he had work done?”

“No.” A shake of the head against the pillow. “Surgery never looks that natural. And besides — when would he find the time? He’s been buried alive in that album.”

“That’s true enough.” Sunny nodded slowly. “No surgery… and it changed anyway. Strange.”

“That’s not the only strange thing, eonni.” Yoon-a sat up too. “I think he’s gotten TALLER.”

“Mwo?”

Sunny went fully alert, and her mind began replaying every frame it possessed of herself standing anywhere near Ji-ho. Nothing conclusive surfaced.

“What makes you say that?”

Yoon-a rubbed her chin, reconstructing the crime scene.

“My barefoot height is 168. On the Alps shoot, I remember it precisely — standing in front of him, I came up to the tip of his nose.”

“Tonight? I only reach the center of his lips.”

She measured the difference in the air between two fingers.

“About two centimeters.”

Sunny’s brain shifted into high gear. If only Taeyeon had grown, coincidence might still cover it. But if HE had grown too — then underneath the coincidence there was a mechanism.

And verification would be trivial. SM logged every trainee’s measurements at intake; the original figures were sitting in a file she could pull the moment she walked into the building. After that, all it took was engineering one small, casual measurement of the current Ji-ho… and comparing.

If he had gained two centimeters as well…

Sunny’s eyes narrowed in the dark. Pleasantly.

✦   ✦   ✦

A small, urgent, quickly-smothered sound crossed the silent apartment — distinct for exactly one second, then gone.

Two women exchanged a deeply embarrassed glance and lay back down in unison.

“Ahem. Sleep.”

Quiet returned. Both of them exhaled. Neither had any appetite left for conversation. Eyes closed; the clock ticked on.

Half an hour passed, give or take.

The same small sound. Unmistakably the same.

“Eonni. Are all men this… durable?” So Yoon-a was still awake after all.

“How would I know?” The whisper came back distinctly aggrieved — you don’t have a man, Yoon-a. Where exactly would I have acquired one?

“Maybe… Ji-ho is simply gifted.”

“Sleep,” Sunny muttered, and hauled the blanket over her own head. Yoon-a copied her exactly.

And at last, the master bedroom went quiet too.

Taeyeon’s self-control was genuinely formidable. But against a Ji-ho running on a system-reinforced constitution, formidable had been proven — on the night’s own evidence — insufficient. Twice.
