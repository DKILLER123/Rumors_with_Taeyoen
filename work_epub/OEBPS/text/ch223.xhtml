Chapter Two Hundred Twenty-Three

Born Aristocrat

July 31 → August 1, 2013 Seoul · midnight forums, a PR war room, two apartments, and one Bentley at dawn a smear campaign lights the fuse — unaware it is standing on the powder

Ji-ho closed the magazine and looked at Ryu Dong-hwan, eyes calm.

“Because he doesn’t dare not to.”

Dong-hwan opened his mouth for the follow-up — and the phone buzzed first. A confirmation message from Lee Myung-ha. Ji-ho glanced at it, and the corner of his mouth rose a millimeter.

Tomorrow. Everything settles tomorrow.

He stood and crossed to the window, watching the traffic stream below.

The building’s gossip, the speculation, the sneers, the worry — he knew all of it, in more detail than any of them would have believed. And he knew something better: when tomorrow’s sun came up, every one of those voices would be singing in a very different key.

“Hyung. Clear my schedule tomorrow morning.”

“Tomorrow morning? What’s the plan?”

“An important appointment.” He turned; the last of the sunset drew a gold line around him. “It concerns — our future.”

Dong-hwan looked at him and felt the strangest double-image: unfamiliar, this scale of audacity and appetite — and utterly familiar, that particular steadiness in the eyes, the everything-is-already-handled look. The same look as when he’d announced he was making The Girl in the Sun. The same look as the English OSTs. And just like that, the manager’s churning stomach went quiet.

“Understood. I’ll arrange it.”

The door clicked shut. Ji-ho turned back to the glass. Seoul’s evening was arriving, neon by neon.

The calm before a storm is always the most beautiful part.

✦ ✦ ✦

Eleven at night. In the corners of a handful of entertainment forums, an unsourced “exposé” surfaced without a sound.

At first, just scattered posts:

Anonymous · entertainment board · 11:04 PM

“LOEN staffer” claims: Ji-ho & Choi Sulli repeatedly seen on intimate dates during The Girl in the Sun shoot

No photos. No recordings. No department, no name, no title for the alleged staffer.

Anonymous · entertainment board · 11:09 PM

Same “insider”: during the OST recordings, Ji-ho was “ambiguous” with Girls’ Generation’s Tiffany AND Taeyeon — a three-boat man?

Again: no evidence attached. The peninsula sees dozens of these a day; most drown before breakfast.

Most drown. This one didn’t. Because this one had paid oars rowing underneath it.

Half past midnight, on one of the biggest entertainment forums, the thread went red-pinned, replies multiplying by the screenful:

Night thread · “LOEN insider spills on Ji-ho??”

17Real or fake??

18Real. My aunt’s daughter’s cousin’s wife’s best friend works at LOEN and she saw them being all intimate herself!!

19Hold on, let me diagram that family tree. WHO saw??

23Forget Sulli — the point is TIFFANY and KIM TAEYEON. Girls’ Generation!! Is Ji-ho insane?

24Three boats at once? If this is real he can retire from the industry today.

31Why do I feel like it’s fake? There’s literally zero proof.

32No proof is exactly what’s suspicious!! If they had receipts they’d post them. You float the rumor first and test reactions. Amateur hour in here.

40“LOEN internal staff”… did Ji-ho make an enemy inside his own company?

One in the morning: the social feeds caught. Several entertainment accounts in the hundreds-of-thousands range reposted within minutes of each other — every headline armored in “allegedly,” “reportedly,” “netizens claim,” and every headline louder than the last.

Aggregator headlines · 1:00–1:30 AM

Rising star Ji-ho in multi-relationship storm? Choi Sulli, Tiffany, Taeyeon named!

Film couple turned real? OST duet sparks? Ji-ho’s love life “a riddle”

“LOEN insider”: Ji-ho “ambiguous” with multiple female artists

The “LOEN insider” tag did the heavy lifting — credibility by association. Add a summer’s worth of rivals happy to shovel coal, and the spark hit the oil drum.

✦ ✦ ✦

Two in the morning. LOEN Entertainment, PR department night-duty room. The phones were a rolling chorus.

“Team leader — media’s calling for an official response—” The young staffer’s hand trembled around the receiver.

“NO response!” Kim Min-su ground it out through her teeth. “One line for every outlet!”

“But—”

“No BUTS!” Her palm cracked the desk. “This comes from upstairs. Am I understood?”

The staffer shrank and obeyed.

Kim Min-su kneaded her temples and cursed Yoo Jae-ha a hundred separate times. THIS was the play? A knife this dirty — and no fear it slips? But she was one small night-shift team leader, and upstairs was upstairs.

“Team leader,” another staffer tried carefully, “shouldn’t we at least alert Ji-ho’s side?”

“Alert them of WHAT?” A cold laugh. “You do exactly what I’ve told you to do. Is that unclear?”

✦ ✦ ✦

Two-thirty. The riverside apartment.

The phone would not stop moving on the nightstand. Ji-ho sat against the headboard, unbothered, thumbing through “exposés” that grew more inventive by the refresh. Dong-hwan had called a dozen-plus times; he hadn’t picked up once. Eventually he just silenced it.

The bathroom door opened. Irene came out toweling her hair, caught him still on the screen, and tilted her head.

“What is it? Who’s looking for you this late?”

“Nothing much.” He set the phone down. “Some boring rumors.”

She crossed to the bed — and stopped mid-motion when she saw the headlines.

“This—” The color dropped out of her face. “Who put this out?”

He drew her down into his arms, took the towel, and worked it gently through her hair.

“It’s fine. Let them run.”

“You’re not going to respond?” Her voice had gone tight against his chest.

“Respond with what?” A soft laugh. “That I’m NOT seeing three women? That you’re the real one?”

Her body stiffened in his arms.

“Relax.” He kissed the top of her head. “By tomorrow, all of it is handled.”

“But— rumors like this hurt the women most. Sulli, and Tiffany sunbae-nim, and Taeyeon sunbae-nim—”

“They’re stronger than you think.” His eyes were unreadable in the dim room. “Besides — in this particular play, they’re cast members too.”

She didn’t understand that. But he’d already switched off the light.

“Sleep. Tomorrow has a good show in it.”

In the dark, Irene listened to the level, unhurried heartbeat under her ear while her own refused to settle. Could it really… be handled?

✦ ✦ ✦

Three in the morning. Yoo Jae-ha’s private villa, the study fogged with cigarette smoke.

“THIS is your masterstroke?” Seo Dae-ha’s face hadn’t recovered its color since the afternoon. “Not one photograph. What wave does this raise?”

“You don’t understand the craft.” Yoo Jae-ha exhaled a ring, expression fat with satisfaction. “No evidence is BETTER. Evidence is a verdict — no evidence is operating room. All the space in the world.”

He waggled his phone, screens of the spreading threads glowing.

“Opinion’s already rolling. Right now Ji-ho’s camp is sweating through the sheets. When he can’t hold anymore, he comes to ME. And I — reluctantly, magnanimously — step out and clarify: it was all promotional strategy for the film and the OSTs. The scandal MADE the heat. Even the shareholders hear a tidy story.”

“And if he doesn’t come?”

“Doesn’t come?” A cold snort. “Then we thicken the soup. A few more ‘insiders,’ juicier details. Commission a couple of think-pieces — fame went to his head, private life in disarray. When it’s boiled long enough, the company steps in FORMALLY: suspension of activities, a period of ‘self-reflection.’”

He flicked ash, eyes glittering dark.

“He needs to learn what an artist IS. However red — a product on the company’s shelf. I decide if you’re famous. I decide if you’re finished.”

Seo Dae-ha watched him and quietly shelved his half-formed warning. Fine. Let him swagger. Yoo Jae-ha had been riding high too long anyway — starting to sit on Seo’s head too. The buyer was a FUND, the rumor said; funds don’t replace CEOs over gossip, and if the CEO stayed, Finance stayed. And if the boy really was the new owners’ knife… well. Watching it shave Yoo Jae-ha’s edges off first wouldn’t be the worst show in Seoul. He arranged his face into loyal concern.

“And the CEO’s office…?”

“The CEO eats and sleeps at SK headquarters these days — no bandwidth for small weather.” A wave of the hand. “Anyway, I’m managing artists on the company’s behalf. He’d endorse it.”

He crushed out the cigarette.

“Relax. This move attacks and retreats on the same legs. Ji-ho—” a dry little laugh, “—can’t turn over the sky.”

✦ ✦ ✦

Six in the morning. First light. The Girls’ Generation dorm.

Nine of them ringed the sofa in various states of just-woken. Yoon-a and Sunny kept sneaking glances — at Taeyeon, then at Tiffany, then back. Tiffany hugged her phone, brows locked. Taeyeon’s face was a closed door.

“Aigoo — what’s everyone staring for?” Yuri broke the silence with both hands thrown wide. “Call the company, have them put out a statement, done.”

“Or Taeyeon just goes public with Ji-ho. You two match anyway.”

“Clarify what, exactly?” Tiffany shook her head. “That there’s ‘nothing’ between us and Ji-ho? With a no-evidence rumor, every clarification is gasoline.”

She hesitated — then looked at Taeyeon, very carefully.

“Does it… bother you?”

Taeyeon was silent a long time.

“What bothers me is that he’s being TARGETED like this.”

Tiffany’s lips parted, then closed, and her head sank. Taeyeon-ah. You really do deserve his love. Thank god — I never once tried to take your place.

✦ ✦ ✦

Seven. Full daylight, the streets filling.

Ji-ho stood on the balcony over the winding gray-green of the Han, phone bright in his hand.

Message · Lee Myung-ha, SHIN & KIM

7:02 AM

Everything is in position. See you in three hours.

He darkened the screen and drew a long breath of the cool morning.

Yoo Jae-ha thought he was fighting an opinion war. He wasn’t. He was providing the overture to a change of dynasty — and paying for the orchestra himself.

He went back inside. Irene was still deep asleep, black hair fanned across the pillow, face untroubled at last. He bent and pressed a kiss to her forehead.

“Good morning, my girls.”

“After today, no one will ever dare use gutter tricks to touch any of you again.”

Then he dressed. The full set, piece by deliberate piece.

DREAM LIFE — WARDROBE EQUIPPED

Ding~ BORN ARISTOCRAT — full set equipped.

SetBlack tailored suit · white shirt · black tie · black leather shoes · haute mechanical watch · gold-rimmed glasses

EffectPresence suppression active · negotiation success up · subordinate loyalty up — scaling with wealth

The mirror no longer shows a top-charting artist. It shows the man who owns the charts.

Seven-thirty. He stepped out of the building’s front door.

A black Bentley waited at the curb; the driver had the rear door open before his second step.

“Mr. Song. Attorney Lee sent me to bring you in.”

He slid into the deep leather, closed his eyes, and let the city move past.

The car ran smooth and silent, straight toward SK Group.
