Chapter One Hundred Sixty-Five

Detective Im, the Little Deer

Late January 2013 · Night A pet shop → the dorm curb → Banpo two detectives invite themselves along

After seeing Director Kwak off, he kept Sulli company at the pet shop.

Small animals dozed and tumbled in their enclosures while “Lucky” — still a snowball of a kitten — sat through the health check curled in Sulli’s arms, periodically butting its head into her palm for maintenance affection.

She gazed down at it, and asked without looking up.

“Oppa. Do you know why I HAD to adopt it?”

“For the second paycheck?”

“Pfft—” The solemnity collapsed; she went pink and delivered a fist to his arm at approximately zero newtons. “I can’t STAND you!~”

He steadied her by the shoulders, and the teasing dropped out of his voice.

“I know what you were going to say. Sulli-yah — don’t think of us as the ones who got left. We’re the free ones. The lucky ones. We get to go out and CHOOSE the people who love us for real. Don’t we?”

She looked up at him and nodded hard, twice.

“Majayo. We’re the free, happy ones.”

Oppa — you always find the exact sentence, right when I’m lowest. I’ve barely needed the medication lately. The doctor says it’s my mood improving. But I know what it actually is. It’s having something to hold onto. It’s you.

He went out front to ask the staff for a printed care sheet, and she trailed him like a small tail, eyes flicking to his arm and back — the urge to just hold it and stroll, filed under NOT YET. In the film, though. In the film she could hold on in broad daylight. Hehe.

✦   ✦   ✦

Across the city, Girls’ Generation finally shut down a very long day and dragged themselves into the van.

Taeyeon opened her phone to one unread message and felt the whole week lift off her shoulders: Nuna, let’s meet tonight. Something important.

She typed back fast — Okay. My place. — and did not notice her own smile.

At the dorm curb, the members peeled off one by one. Taeyeon hung to the back of the line, drafting exit strategies.

“Um — I have something on. Not coming up.” Sooyoung, with mysterious dignity.

Sooyoung’s dating life was a half-open secret; nobody blinked.

“I’m hitting a bar — anyone in?” Hyoyeon’s hand shot up on her own question. She scanned the group and targeted the survivors. “Sica. Tiffany. No morning schedule tomorrow. One drink.”

Jessica visibly wavered. Tiffany shook her head — bars had lost their charm for her after the last incident. Hyoyeon, whose reformation had lasted a commendable few months, was officially back.

Taeyeon raised a careful hand.

“I’m… also not coming up. You all rest well.”

Jessica gave her a strange look. The homebody of the century, going OUT?

And one member who had been running on empty came suddenly, vibrantly online. Sunny locked onto Taeyeon’s arm like a customs officer.

“Taeyeon-ah~ I miss your bed SO much. Tonight I’m coming with you, okay~?”

“Eh?”

Sunny. You KNOW where I’m going. Why are you doing this to me?

Before she could regroup, a second operative attached to her other arm, sweet as syrup.

“Right, right! Eonni’s bed is the softest one I’ve ever slept in. Me too.”

“Really?” Yuri’s radar for fun pinged awake. “Then I want to try it too.”

“Why don’t we ALL just drink at Taeyeon’s,” Jessica proposed, eyes rolling with mischief.

“Yes!! I’ve never seen eonni’s place!”

“Housewarming! It counts as a housewarming!”

“ANDWAE!!!!” Taeyeon’s veto came out at fire-alarm volume, panic showing at the edges. “I— I suddenly don’t want to go home. So tired. Just want to sleep.”

She actually attempted a retreat toward the dorm. Sunny — who had already pulled his intake file and knew the registered height read 182 where the tape measure now said 184 — had no intention of losing this rare access window, and equally no intention of sharing it with a crowd. She clamped down on Taeyeon’s arm, pried Yoon-a’s hand off the other one, and towed the leader bodily toward the garage.

“Who does a housewarming at MIDNIGHT? I need Taeyeon for work matters. The rest of you do the housewarming some other weekend.”

Yoon-a’s eyes narrowed to pleasant crescents that were not pleasant at all. She had happened to overhear Sunny requesting a certain someone’s file from the trainee-affairs office — and, purely out of curiosity, had happened to read it after Sunny finished. And found the same arithmetic: 184 measured in the Hong Kong hotel, 182 in a file dated June. Two centimeters in four months, at age nineteen. And the leader’s own file now read 162. There was a secret in this somewhere — and Sunny’s tugboat act confirmed she’d found the same trail. Hnn. The clever little deer sees EVERYTHING.

The remaining members scattered to dates and beds. Jessica noticed Yoon-a rooted to the pavement.

“Yoon-a. Not coming up?”

“Eonni, go ahead~ I have a little errand.”

“Weird. Everyone’s being SO weird lately,” Jessica muttered, scratching her head.

“What’s weird? Sica — come drink with me.” Hyoyeon had her by the arm already. “I’ll introduce you to some friends.”

Jessica hesitated — she DID want a drink, but after the last mess she wanted nothing to do with Hyoyeon’s friend circle. Yoon-a, who liked that circle even less, produced an inspiration at broadcast volume:

“Sica eonni! My errand’s near a shopping mall — come with me? We could look at perfume.”

“Perfume?” Instant illumination. “Okay. Hyoyeon, drink without me.”

In the car, seatbelt clicking, Jessica asked the natural question.

“So where’s this mall?”

“Taeyeon eonni’s apartment.”

“MWO?”

✦   ✦   ✦

He’d dropped Sulli home, returned to the studio, and put in hours on the film score. In a break he checked the brokerage account — and found the Tokyo Electric position up forty percent.

He couldn’t touch the Wealth Robot’s sealed machine, but its trade log was readable — and Tokyo Electric kept appearing in it. So he’d opened a small separate account of his own and parked five hundred million idle won on the same name. Under a month later: seven hundred million. Equities were a fine servant and a catastrophic master; it paid to be the one reading a machine’s homework.

Taeyeon’s schedule would be ending about now. He shut the console and drove for Banpo.

There was a specific convenience to a girlfriend who was rich, busy, and a committed homebody: her sanctuary was her own apartment, which meant zero risk of her materializing at HIS — where a certain other someone’s toothbrush and slippers now lived. He’d also expanded the garage: a BMW for daylight, a Mercedes for disguised nights. Tonight was a Mercedes night.

He hadn’t seen her in too long, and the drive felt slow. At her door he keyed the code from muscle memory.

Beep-beep-beep-beep.

“Ji-ho’s here!”

From the sofa, Taeyeon launched like a spring lamb and bolted for the entryway. Sunny watched the takeoff, shaking her head — tsk tsk tsk, THAT smitten? — and followed. In the kitchen, Yoon-a’s ramyeon-stirring accelerated noticeably. And beside her, conscripted as kitchen supervisor, Jessica stood entirely lost: WHO just let themselves in with the door code?

The door swung open, and the first thing he saw was Taeyeon at a full sprint — house clothes, a flash of bare waist, a smile you could read from orbit.

He was smiling back before he decided to.

She hit his chest at speed, and he gathered her up — soft, warm, faintly sweet — and looked down at the flushed, delighted face, and kissed her without a single strategic thought in his head.

Her eyes flew wide. THE MEMBERS ARE RIGHT THERE— but his mouth was familiar and sure, and the resistance lasted roughly the duration of one heartbeat before body and mind both filed their surrender. Fine. FINE. Let them watch and be jealous. That’s what they get for tagging along uninvited. May they all develop styes.

Jessica stood in the kitchen doorway, wooden spoon in hand, watching the entryway with the expression of a woman whose entire evening had stopped making sense. Since WHEN does Taeyeon have a boyfriend? And why are they EATING each other in the doorway?

Yoon-a and Sunny, meanwhile, had arranged themselves side by side with the settled comfort of a matinee audience, eyes shining.

“Tsk tsk tsk. SO sweet.”

“Eww~ so cheesy.”

“Wah, wah — as expected of our Taeyeon. Where exactly is that hand going?”
