Chapter Sixty-Seven

What “With Your Own Lips” Means

He drew her in — the last centimeter of distance closing — and bent his head, and kissed her.

Softness beyond reason. The sweet scent flooding in; the small warm shape of her folding against him; his arms tightening without instruction.

With… with my own lips — THAT’S what he meant—

She stood wide-eyed and frozen, those beautiful eyes cycling through alarm, shyness, and something with a much faster pulse — staring at the face a breath away, close enough to count the fine grain of his skin, to feel the rhythm of his breathing against hers.

He tasted peach lip balm first — clean, light, like fruit straight off the tree — and beneath it the lingering richness of strawberry gummies, sweet without heaviness.

And she — brain empty, body entirely off-script — found herself leaning in rather than away, her hands arriving at his shoulders without any memory of the journey.

Seconds passed, or minutes; the stairwell declined to keep time. One of his hands slid up through the soft fall of her hair to cradle the slim back of her neck; the other traced down the line of her waist and settled at the small of her back, resting there, thumb moving in one slow, careful arc. The warmth of it finally reached whatever part of Taeyeon was still taking notes—

FACE. ON. FIRE.

She shoved at his chest — and this time he yielded instantly, hands falling away, a full palm’s width of air restored between them.

“Ji-ho… you… you—”

“I—”

Her thoughts were a dropped drawer of cutlery. My first kiss. That was my FIRST kiss. We’re not even dating — how could he just— how DARE he just—

He stepped forward, once, unhurried.

“Just now — I was happy. Were you not?”

A beat.

“Because of the kiss? Or because of the person?”

Field Notes

Ambiguity never holds. As contact compounds and fondness ferments, every almost-couple arrives at the same three-way fork: someone names the feeling and the other accepts — the paper window torn, the real thing begun. Or someone flinches, contact thins, and the whole season files itself away as a wistful parenthesis. Or both keep silent, and the ambiguity simply outlives its own freshness, cooling by degrees into nothing at all.

He was certain of her. The kiss had met no resistance — had been, in its shy and unpracticed way, answered.

“That’s… that’s not what I—”

Her gaze skittered — and betrayed her completely by dropping, one involuntary beat, to his mouth: faintly wet, a shade redder than usual, carrying her lip color.

The kiss had lasted a minute at least. His lips had felt — fine. Better than fine. His hands had made her heart go soft at the edges. Someone had once told her: a woman only knows whether she loves a man after she’s kissed him. Standing in the stairwell with her ears burning, Taeyeon now possessed the data. She liked this person. Body and heart had both already voted; the count wasn’t close.

The realization made her ears burn hotter.

“Good.”

He smiled — leaned in once more — and pressed a kiss, feather-quick, to her scalding cheek, right on the full curve of it.

Twenty-four centimeters of height difference gave her all the time in the world to dodge. She stood rooted like a spellbound thing instead, and let it land.

Silence stretched through the stairwell. Then, stammering, flustered, splendidly incoherent:

“That— um— there’s a music show stage tomorrow. I— mm— I’m exhausted, and if I’m any later I’ll miss the skin-regeneration window Seohyun keeps talking about—”

“Understood, nuna.” His voice had gone very gentle. “Goodnight.”

“…Goodnight.”

And she turned and fled the stairwell like weather escaping a front, gone in seconds.

Yoon-a, mid-banana-cookie, raised one in greeting.

“Eonni, do you—”

Taeyeon shot past without a glance, straight into her room.

BANG!

The door slam startled Yoon-a into finishing the sentence to an empty hallway.

“…want a cookie.”

“YAH! How many times — CLOSE DOORS GENTLY!” Jessica’s door flew open, face full of thunderclouds. “Who just came home?!”

“…Taeyeon eonni,” Yoon-a reported, in the tone of someone declining to be collateral damage.

Taeyeon? Jessica’s irritation downshifted into something else — concern. That wasn’t a Taeyeon-shaped entrance. She pushed into the room; Yoon-a pattered in after.

Inside: Taeyeon, face-down on her bed, face buried in the pillow, shoulders shaking with smothered laughter, both feet swinging in the air with incriminating joy.

Sunny sat up in the opposite bed, startled by the door.

“Taeyeon-ah… what happened to you?”

Jessica and Yoon-a arrived at the bedside; three sets of eyes conferred.

Who upset her? — No idea. — Even LESS idea.

Taeyeon rolled upright, assembling her face into something resembling normal.

“It’s nothing. Just — music shows back to back. I’m tired, that’s all.”

Jessica bought it at face value and rolled her eyes.

“Fine, fine — we KNOW your little unit’s doing well. Aigoo. So jealous.”

(Resource envy was eternal in any idol group; Girls’ Generation simply ranked among the few who’d survived enough together — Black Ocean included — to joke about it freely.)

“Your drama’s doing fine too~” Taeyeon batted back, eyes crinkling. “Aigoo, a ROMANCE~ so romantic~ When are we all watching it together? Contribute some ratings for our Sica?”

Direct hit. Jessica flushed scarlet on the spot.

“YAH! NOBODY is watching it!”

“I’m watching it right now.” Phone already out, delighted beyond measure.

“YAH! AN-DWAE!!”

Jessica launched; the two of them went down in a tangle of blankets and shrieking, wrestling for the phone.

And off to the side, Sunny and Yoon-a stood with folded arms, running a quieter analysis. Taeyeon could fool Jessica. She could not fool them. Tired? Tired? The woman’s mouth-corners were physically unable to descend. Her eyes had gone to crescents and stayed there. Her earlobes were pink. Her gaze kept floating off to the middle distance and returning with a smile attached.

First Soo-young. Now Taeyeon. Girls’ Generation is quietly entering its dating era…

Mid-wrestle, Taeyeon’s phone chimed. KakaoTalk. At this hour—

Strength from nowhere: she flung Jessica off, rolled to her feet, and was gone through the door in one motion.

“I’m showering and going to BED!”

She locked herself in the bathroom and opened the message. Him. Of course him.
