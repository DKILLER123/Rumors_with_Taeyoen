Her fingers tightened in the sheet.

The last few minutes · suddenly unbearable to remember

She had been so excited. Pulling Seohyun closer. Demanding details. Running through handsome actors as though selecting the right one would complete a particularly entertaining evening.

Who was the lucky man?

Now she had an answer, and the answer had been walking into her own home.

Had she really been enjoying gossip about herself?

The absurdity almost made her laugh. Almost. Crying did not seem possible either.

What is this supposed to be?

Anger?Surely. She had forced herself to accept Fany. Now that bastard had gone after Yoon-a as well.
Hurt?That, too. He had come all the way here without contacting her. He had gone to Yoon-a instead.
Humiliation?Perhaps most of all. Her own eager questions. Her own delighted face. The fool she had been while she guessed.

Choosing a word for it did not make the others go away.

She closed her eyes and drew a careful breath.

An attempt at reason · addressed to herself

Calm down, Kim Taeyeon.

What if it wasn’t him? What if this was a coincidence?

There were plenty of tall men in entertainment. Plenty with beautiful eyes. A familiar back did not have to belong to…

She could produce all the objections. None of them sounded convincing in her own head.

A reputation doing its work · the conclusion she cannot dismiss

It is him

THE ANSWERApparently this was what a reputation meant. If it was him, nothing about it was surprising.

She would have liked to be surprised. Surprise would have meant she had expected better.

The detail that hurts · no longer Seohyun’s amusing evidence

Swollen lips. A kiss that must have been quite something.

Taeyeon knew how he kissed. The tenderness, the lingering closeness, the way it became difficult to remember anything outside it.

Now she imagined him kissing Yoon-a that way.

Her grip on the sheet tightened again. It was no use telling herself that she had not seen it.

She lost track of how long she sat there. The others were still drinking; nobody came to disturb her.

Usually that would have suited her perfectly. Tonight, being left alone was intolerable.

The hotel door · a journey abandoned before it begins She got up abruptly and crossed the room. Her hand closed around the handle. Where was she going? To the hospital? She stopped. The room she might enter was already taking shape in her mind. And then what? Open the door and find him beside the bed, holding Yoon-a’s hand? Or… She could not follow the thought. Her fingers loosened. With her back against the door, she slowly slid to the floor.

She drew her knees up, buried her face against them, and began to shake.

A moment ago, she had wanted every detail. Now she could not bear to imagine the room.
