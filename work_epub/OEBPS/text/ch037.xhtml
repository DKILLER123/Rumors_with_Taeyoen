Charm 50!

Ji-ho never noticed Baekhyun at all — never knew he’d casually bulldozed the man’s one assembled moment of courage. The twenty-minute break evaporated, and the final setup was called.

Center stage: a white grand piano, staircases sweeping down on either side, a minimalist starfield glowing across the backdrop, the lighting tuned soft and dreamlike. TaeTiSeo waited in the wings in matching gowns of different colors. The director crossed to Ji-ho and clapped his shoulder.

“Ji-ho — this scene, you’re playing live. If you can actually play, give me anything you like. If you can’t, fake it quietly — just don’t hit anything so cursed it breaks the ladies mid-take.”

Ji-ho settled onto the bench and drew a fingertip across the keys — one bright ripple of sound. He looked up with a smile that had a whole professional-grade piano skill behind it.

“Understood, Director-nim.”

“Good. Rolling! All departments — standby!”

Silence dropped over the floor.

Performance

— breath in. Hands to the keys. The first phrase opens like a door — A slow, tender prelude: melody soft-spoken, confiding, a story told at close range. Then the tempo gathers — the line turns soaring, infectious, every note striking dead center of the beat, the tone full and warm and carrying to the rafters. On the right staircase, Taeyeon lifts her hem and descends — and at the first bars her step catches, surprise flashing through her eyes. She follows the sound to the boy at the piano: head bowed, wholly absorbed, fingers running the keys like water finding stones. On the left staircase, Tiffany’s eyebrows have opinions of their own. She and Taeyeon trade one look: the same astonishment, mirrored. At center stage, Seohyun’s choreography adjusts itself to the live line without her deciding anything — her body simply agreeing with the music, looser and freer than any rehearsal. Taeyeon reaches the piano and takes her scripted place at its edge, dancing — soft, fluid — and her eyes keep drifting to him anyway. The playing fits her movement like a score written to her measurements. Tiffany and Seohyun join at center. Three dancers, one live piano, every turn and lifted hand landing inside the music’s breath.

The final chord settled. The floor held silent for a few seconds — then the crew’s applause broke out across the soundstage.

“That was GORGEOUS! Ji-ho, what even ARE you?!” Pure delight in Tiffany’s voice.

“Seriously,” Seohyun nodded, still blinking. “I had no idea you played like that. None.”

“That was professional level. Fully.” Taeyeon’s gaze had open admiration in it now. “Where did THAT come from?”

He rose from the bench, scratched the back of his head, and produced a modesty so transparent you could read a newspaper through it.

“Thank you, nunas. It just… happens to be a thing I’m good at.”

The words were humble. The glint in his eye had already ordered champagne. And watching him stand there — humble and insufferable in perfect balance — the same thought crossed three minds at once: this boy is a treasure chest with legs. Every single time, another compartment opens. What’s NEXT?

His coverage complete, Ji-ho headed home; TaeTiSeo stayed behind for pickups.
