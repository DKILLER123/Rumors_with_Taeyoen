“Comfortable?”

“Hateful!”

She pushed him off, sat up wrapped in the quilt like a formally dressed empress, and pointed one arm at the window where the sun was now fully on shift.

He went, laughing under his breath, toward the bathroom. She sat in the wrecked sheets and watched his back the whole way there, and the smile on her face declined every instruction to leave. She inspected her own forearm. She patted her own cheek. The smile held.

Good skin. Good mood. Good body. And the headline item: present, accounted for, and mine. The morning has no complaints filed.

Bare feet, his shirt, a pad down the cold marble; she came into the bathroom and attached herself to his back while he brushed, arms locked at the waist, cheek between his shoulder blades.

“Hm?” he managed, around the toothbrush.

He met her eyes in the mirror, and the small face looking back at him was incandescent with contentment, and his eyes did the thing they only did when nobody official was watching.
