That night, Taeyeon sat cross-legged on her dorm bed, tablet propped up, watching Sketchbook live to the second.

The moment his face filled the screen, her mouth curved without asking permission.

Through the English ballad she hummed along under her breath, eyes going liquid. As expected of MY nam-chin. The singing got better AGAIN.

Then he said it — a teacher helped me, and shared her own story — and her eyes flew wide, and she laughed like a child handed candy.

The production room. That was US. I gave notes on the lyrics AND the arrangement, and I taught him vocals — “teacher” is entirely accurate.

A secret spoken in public, legible to exactly two people on Earth — like trading passwords over enemy radio. She hugged her knees, delighted beyond all proportion.

The second mention — the detailed account of the evaluation — pinked her face and warmed her straight through. She remembered the day precisely: nineteen-year-old Ji-ho, nervous and dead serious, and that grain in his voice that made her think, this trainee debuts. Guaranteed.

“At least you have a conscience. You remembered who gave you that perfect score.” She picked up her phone to message him — then put it down. “After the show.”

And when he finished narrating his audition saga, she knew exactly what he was doing. His non-dance evaluations had all been high; the perfect vocal score had never been the reason he’d made trainee. Praising me THAT openly, on national broadcast… aren’t you afraid of misunderstandings, you reckless man? The smile refused to be suppressed.

Then the program reached the “Some” duet stage.

And her expression began, degree by degree, to change.

IU took his hand — naturally, smoothly, on camera.

“Mwo? YAH!”

“Aish— HANDS OFF!”

The two of them smiled into each other’s eyes under pink light. Taeyeon’s teeth found her lower lip.

“Deep soulful eye contact, is it. Ji-ho. You are DEAD.”

And the lyric — the one about wanting to wake up every morning to that person’s message — paired with all that fluent, practiced interplay, pulled something taut in her chest. She inhaled slowly and administered first aid to herself: Staging. It’s ALL staging.

She kept her eyes nailed to the screen anyway. Not one frame unattended. IU’s gaze was too expressive. His answering smile was too gentle.

Do they look at each other like that in PRACTICE too?

And the worst part — when IU’s hand found his, his fingers closed back around hers. Naturally. Only for a few seconds. But back.

The credits rolled. She stared at the dead black screen for several minutes.

Then she snatched up her phone and called him.

Outgoing · Ji-ho

“Hello — nuna?” Background noise; a schedule just ending.

“I watched the show.” Voice pressed carefully flat. “Not bad. The English ballad was very good.”

“Really? Thank you, nuna. I was so nervous every time I mentioned you — terrified of misspeaking.”

“You spoke… fine.” A pause. Studied carelessness: “Though — the stage with IU. You two must have practiced a LOT. Such good chemistry.”

A soft laugh down the line. “Nuna. Are you… jealous, by any chance?”

“MWO? Jealous of WHAT!” — instantly, several decibels too loud — “I am simply monitoring my boyfriend’s stage performance in my capacity AS his girlfriend!”

“Yes, yes.” Audible suppressed laughter. “We rehearsed about a week. IU nuna has years of stage experience — she helped me a lot. She’s genuinely a good person—”

“YAH, you little—!” The dam went. “FINE. Yes. I’m jealous. My entire room currently smells like vinegar. What are you going to DO about it?”

“How will you take responsibility, you BAD boy!”

“Hahaha — nuna, you’re really cute.”

“…Mwo?”

Her heart, at that one sentence, broke into a gallop — and the rest of her went soft as warmed butter.

“I said: nuna. You. Are. Really. Cute.”

“Hehehe.” Full idiot-grin, unwitnessed, unashamed. “Ani, I mean — obviously I AM very cute, but you can’t just KEEP it in your mouth like that~ it’s embarrassing.”

“Say what you feel, when you feel it.” His voice was warm. “Like is like. Jealous is jealous. And nuna is cute. That’s all.”

Little by little, he kept doing this — coaxing the doors open, so she’d stop twisting everything up and hiding it inside.

A short silence on her end.

“…Understood,” she answered, soft as down. Then, abruptly: “When do you rest?”

“Mm~ probably when promotions wrap. Nuna? Aren’t you busy lately?”

“However busy, I can MAKE time.” Iron certainty — followed by a mutter dropped to near-zero volume: “…before you sing any more love songs with other women…”

“What was that?”

“NOTHING! Anyway — that story today, failing twelve auditions. Was that true?”

✦   ✦   ✦

They talked a while longer. After the call ended, she sat unmoving, thinking.

She looked around the Girls’ Generation dorm — warm, familiar, and irreducibly shared. A member could walk in at any moment. Since Jeonju, she and Ji-ho hadn’t managed to meet even once. The schedules were the lesser problem. The real problem was geometry: there was nowhere to be. Their entire relationship, physically speaking, currently fit inside a parked car.

“This won’t do…”

She thought of tonight’s broadcast — IU and Ji-ho, publicly side by side, collaborating in the open air. She knew it was work. The envy arrived anyway: that daylight. If she and Ji-ho could stand on a stage like that—

She shook the thought off. Two years. At least two more years.

But at minimum — they deserved one room of their own. One place with no schedules listening through the walls, where two people could simply be a couple.

She picked up her phone and opened a real-estate app, and this time her browsing was not idle.

Real Estate · Saved Listings

tonight · 1:12 AM

Nonhyeon-dong — good… Cheongdam-dong — worth considering… oh, Hannam-dong has a new luxury build…

[ 4 screenshots saved · memo: contact agent tomorrow ]

Her eyes steadied into resolve. Quickly. This has to happen quickly.

The tent came back to her — that night, that feeling.

I miss you, Ji-ho. I want to be held so badly it aches. A house. There MUST be a house.

The thought of a small future space where the two of them could waste quiet hours together put the smile back on her face. She screenshotted her favorites, resolved to call the agent first thing—

—and the screen lit with a new message: Nuna, I should have free time mid-month. Meet?

She typed back a single syllable — Yes — and hugged the phone to her chest, faint pink rising. Buy the apartment BEFORE his break. And then~ hehe.

This time, she was taking the step herself. For her own happiness. Proactively.

Because watching IU stand that close — the crisis alarm would not stop ringing. Her Ji-ho was TOO excellent. What if Ji-eun fell for him? He’d only ever love me, obviously. But what if she INSISTED on seducing him? Entirely plausible. Loving him is as easy as breathing — that face, that talent. No. Unacceptable. Next week’s “Show! Music Core” — I’m hosting. I will be DEVASTATING. And I will, ever so slightly, plant a flag where Ji-eun can see it.

Ji-eun. The song — fine. Luck of the draw, and his debut did ride your popularity. I’ll allow it. But the MAN is mine.

✦   ✦   ✦

Ji-ho hung up and laughed to himself. He missed her too.

Right. Home tonight: squat rack and ab board. Extra sets.

The schedule had ended early for once. Back at the apartment, he put the God of Learning to work on finance textbooks. Money was arriving now — but buying LOEN in two years on artist income alone was arithmetic fantasy. From here on, the portfolio mattered as much as the discography.
