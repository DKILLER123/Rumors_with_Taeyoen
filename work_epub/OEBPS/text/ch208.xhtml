Chapter Two Hundred Eight

Cuddles Only

The same night The villa borrowed slippers, borrowed pajamas — and a small arsonist who only starts fires

The garage door rolled down and sealed the night outside.

Sulli followed him into the villa — and her small nose twitched at once, cataloguing the air like an animal confirming territory: a faint layered residue of several perfumes, none of them hers.

“Smells nice in here.” Said lightly. Meanwhile her eyes made one unhurried sweep of the living room — tidy, but the coffee table still held a small congress of mismatched cups.

He hung his jacket. “The Girls’ Generation nunas came by last night. Housewarming, allegedly.”

“Ohh~” The syllable stretched three beats long. She clasped her hands behind her back and trailed after him, footsteps light.

Taeyeon eonni. Truly a woman of fortune. — But. A small hum of satisfaction underneath: tonight, Oppa is MINE.

From the shoe cabinet she excavated a pair of unmistakably feminine fluffy slippers and stepped into them without one millisecond of hesitation. Slightly large; her toes wiggled experimentally in the extra room.

Taeyeon eonni’s. Big-foot eonni.

Then she drifted to his side with the ease of long habit and tipped her face up. “Oppa. Do you have pajamas I can wear?”

He gave her a look, went to the bedroom closet, and came back with a clean silk set — women’s. Also Taeyeon’s.

“Taeyeon nuna wore these last night. If that bothers you, I’ll dig out something of mine.”

“I don’t mind at all.” She gathered the silk against her chest, and the smile went one degree sweeter.

When she emerged — washed, bare-faced, hair damp, wrapped in silk that fit her taller frame a little too closely — he was out of the shower himself, reaching for water, and forgot mid-reach to stop looking.

Sulli without makeup was arguably the best bare face he had ever seen: skin white and fine past reason, poreless, jade-luminous; natural tidy brows; eyes still bright but scrubbed of all stagecraft, leaving something younger and cleaner; lips their own soft pink. The silk followed her lines and gave up the collarbones; wet black hair dripped at the tips; sleeves shoved up over forearms of blinding white. The total effect was fresh and quietly ruinous.

She swayed over to him. “Last night must have been LIVELY, hm? The Girls’ Generation eonnis are a whole festival.”

“It was fine.” He cracked the bottle and held it out.

She didn’t take it. She leaned in instead and drank a small sip straight from the bottle in his hand — lips wet, eye-corners curling, gaze hooked into his the whole time.

“So~ tonight it’s just us two. Oppa won’t find it too QUIET, will he?”

She was close now, wrapped in bath-warmth — faint milk-sweetness over her own skin-scent, arriving in slow waves.

He didn’t move. Her face at this range was absurd; even the fine down on her cheek looked deliberate.

“And how exactly do you propose to make it un-quiet?” His voice had dropped a register.

The smile in her eyes went fully vulpine — and then two ice-cold hands dove under the hem of his shirt and flattened themselves against his stomach.

“YAH— your hands are FREEZING—”

He jolted, grabbing for the offending wrists — cool, smooth, and entirely unrepentant.

“Then Oppa should warm them~”

She was giggling, body slipping eel-close against him, face tipped up, nose grazing his cheek — breath tangling, lashes fluttering at close range, lips soft and damp and frankly advertising.

He wrapped her waist and bent for her mouth.

She dodged — a half-turn of the head, her lips skating his cheek instead, hot breath landing directly in his ear.

“What’s the hurry, Oppa~”

Sticky-sweet, while a fingertip drew lazy circles over his abdomen.

“Choi Sulli.” Full name. Warning grade. His arms tightened.

Only then did she laugh and come up to meet him.

Cool soft lips, toothpaste-mint. Light pecks first — then, under his weight and heat, warmer, deeper, more entangled. Her hands wandered up from his stomach and began, with luxurious lack of urgency, unbuttoning his shirt. His palm slid under silk and up the satin line of her back — a texture that earned a sigh — then drifted, slow, toward the front.

Her breath broke rhythm; a small sound escaped into the kiss; she pressed closer, kissing him like something she intended to finish—

His fingertips reached the waistband’s edge.

And froze — because at exactly that moment, she bit down on his lower lip, hard enough to mean it, and slipped back out of range with her eyes glittering, triumph and mischief at a rolling boil.

She licked her own kiss-brightened lips and burst out laughing.

“Mianhae, Oppa~” She leaned to his ear, and the whisper poured in warm and merciless. “It seems like— I’m not exactly convenient tonight.”

And then she lost her own composure entirely — collapsed against his shoulder giggling, shoulders shaking, a small cat vibrating with the success of its crime.

He stood there with the fire she’d built still burning and nowhere to put it — furious, amused, mostly amused, mostly not.

He moved to peel the little arsonist off him.

“Yah— you little—”

“Mmn~”

The push had the opposite effect: she went boneless and re-attached at a tighter tolerance, arms locked around his neck, whole self suspended from him, cheek burrowing into the crook of his neck, voice going full syrup.

“Oppa don’t be mad~ I didn’t do it on PURPOSE~ really! I swear!”

An oath with the structural integrity of candy floss, tail-note swung all the way up into aegyo.

“Down.”

“Won’t~” A wriggle. Full tenancy asserted. “Tonight I’m sleeping holding Oppa. PURE sleeping! I’ll be SO well-behaved!”

She lifted her head and deployed the eyes — enormous, transparent, engineered for innocence — while the corner of her mouth flatly refused to decommission its smirk.

He looked down at the warm weight in his arms — the bare face pure and lethal at once, the shameless clinging, the whole sweet-tart-green-tea calibration of her, dosed to the milligram — and felt the anger dissolve without ever having been consulted. What remained was the usual ruinous fondness.

He sighed, arms shifting to carry her properly, and turned for the bedroom.

“Oppa is the BEST!”

Victory declared, she smacked a loud kiss onto his cheek and settled her head onto his shoulder, two oversized slippers swinging happily off her toes.

✦   ✦   ✦

The bed was wide and soft. They lay side by side — and she assumed her octopus configuration instantly, all four limbs committed. But true to her word: only holding. Genuinely, deliberately well-behaved.

Beauty in his arms; fire still banked and unpaid. Sleep declined to attend.

He kissed the top of her head. “Be good. I’m getting something.”

She released him obediently. He returned with the tablet and sat back against the headboard. She promptly re-materialized at his shoulder.

“Oppa, what are you looking at?”

“Preview reactions. The critics.”

“How is it? Are the ones praising ME numerous?”

The eyes over his shoulder were enormous and dewy.

“Mm. They say your acting is vivid. Alive.”

“Obviously~”

She scrunched her nose in triumph, then poured herself into his lap region by degrees, arm around his waist, boneless, face upturned — and the look in her eyes now was pure, uncut temptation of the artisanal kind.

“Then~ does Oppa think I’m pretty?”

One fingertip, drawing idle circles on his chest.

He set the tablet down and regarded her with the weariness of a man being robbed slowly. Does this girl KNOW what she looks like? — Of course she knows. That’s the entire strategy. Sleep has been formally canceled.

“Pretty,” he sighed.

She beamed like a paid endorsement, looped his neck, plastered her silk-wrapped self one layer closer, and breathed orchid-warm against his jaw.

“Then~ shouldn’t Oppa look a little more CAREFULLY?”

Eyes closed. Lashes trembling. Lips pursed in soft, explicit petition.

His eyes darkened. His hand cupped the back of her skull, and he kissed her properly.

She answered with enthusiasm and zero remorse — arms tight, body restless in his lap, silk conducting every movement with treasonous fidelity. The kiss deepened; his palm ran the long line of her waist through fabric gone warm; his breathing thickened—

He released her only when she whimpered — cheeks flushed to a dangerous shade, eyes glazed — and he was breathing like a man post-sprint.

“Little menace. Stop lighting fires.”

“Behave.”

He set her firmly back at arm’s length — a quarantine measure.

“YAH! Don’t push me away~!”

The quarantine failed in under two seconds: she re-adhered like taffy, both arms around his bicep, full weight committed, head grinding a slow burrow into his shoulder, voice dissolving into glutinous nonsense.

“I just want to STAY like this a while~ kisses and hugs aren’t illegal~ Oppa is warm~ so comfortable~”

And all through it, one eye kept sliding up to check his expression — found no genuine anger anywhere in it — and the campaign escalated accordingly: cheek against his jaw, fingertip poking his abs, an unbroken murmured filibuster of shameless nothing. She had lit the fire, and now stood beside it in full little-sister innocence, wanting only cuddles. The dosage between pure, seductive, and green-tea was calibrated to the microgram. It was, honestly, a virtuoso performance.

He gave up. Formally. Unconditionally. She hung off him koala-style, kissing and patting whatever was in range, and he flicked her forehead once with two knuckles.

“Don’t push your luck.”

“Ne~!”

Sung out loud and bright — and thoroughly satisfied, she performed one final tenancy adjustment, wedged herself into the optimal hollow of him, and left one hand tracing idle, proprietary lines across his stomach.

Hehe. Oppa’s build is genuinely excellent.

He retrieved the tablet with his free arm and went back to the reviews.

Some minutes later, a light, even breathing settled against his neck.

The cool ghost of her fingers still on his stomach; her clean scent in every breath he took. He glanced down at the sleeping face — the corner of her mouth still holding its smile — and finally smiled too, pulled her in one degree closer, and closed his eyes.

And Sulli — asleep, or near enough — seemed to sense it somehow.

The smile at her lips went deeper. And sweeter.
