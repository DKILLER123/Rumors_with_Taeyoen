Chapter Three Hundred Seven

How to Win Taeyeon’s Forgiveness

October 5, 2013 · Saturday morning Singapore · Mount Elizabeth Novena Hospital

The elevator doors closed, and Taeyeon’s face vanished into the narrowing gap.

By the time Ji-ho reached them, the floor indicator was already changing. Three. Two.

He hammered the down button. The other elevator remained stubbornly on the eighth floor.

“Aish.”

He spun toward the stairs and shoved through the fire door. It banged behind him, his footsteps crashing through the empty stairwell.

Three steps at a time, one hand sliding along the rail, he raced down from the third floor to the second, then the first.

He burst into the ground-floor lobby and looked at the indicator.

B1.

She’d come by car?!

Ji-ho wheeled around and ran back to the stairs, heading for the parking garage.

⁂

In the hospital room, Tiffany folded her arms. Her expression was cold.

“Satisfied now?”

Yoon-a wiped away her tears, looking surprisingly composed. “What do you mean, eonni? I don’t understand.”

“You don’t understand?” Tiffany’s laugh was sharp. “Stop pretending, Im Yoon-a. You knew Taeyeon and I were with him. Why did you have to get involved?”

“Why did I get involved…?”

Yoon-a repeated it softly, as though asking herself.

Because three of her eonnis had gone before her? Because he was so exceptional? Because of that indefinable something about him?

There were too many reasons. But the moment she had truly fallen was yesterday, when he appeared in her hospital room.

She couldn’t imagine any woman resisting what she had felt then.

“You’re a woman too, eonni. You should understand. The moment he walked into my room, I was finished.” She sighed.

“I know it’s wrong. I know he’s Taeyeon eonni’s boyfriend, and I know about you and him. But I couldn’t help it.”

“Couldn’t help it?” Tiffany’s voice rose. “Are you three years old? What do you mean, you couldn’t help it?”

“What are we to each other? We debuted together, lived together, spent all those years laughing and crying together. We’re sisters!”

“Yes. We’re the closest of sisters—”

“So you do remember! If you really cared about that, you’d stay away from him. You should have stopped this the moment you realized you liked him!”

“Fany eonni.”

Yoon-a’s sudden calm was almost unnatural.

“What about you?”

Tiffany froze.

“When did it start for you?” Yoon-a watched her closely. “Before you knew about him and Taeyeon eonni? After? Did you stop yourself?”

The room went terribly quiet.

“I…” For the first time, Tiffany’s voice lost its certainty. “I’m different.”

“How? Because I came later? Because you think being first gives you more right to him?”

Yoon-a did not let her look away.

“Look me in the eye, eonni. Do you honestly think you’re better than I am?”

“You and I are in exactly the same position. He wrote songs for you. He gave you a villa. You chose him.”

“There are things I want too. I really don’t understand what gives you the right to condemn me.”

Tiffany’s face darkened. She pointed at Yoon-a, her hand trembling.

“Mwo? The same? How are you anything like me?”

“I liked him before I even knew he was with Taeyeon, when he was just a trainee!”

“I thought I’d wait. Let him debut. Wait until there wasn’t such a huge gap between us. And what happened? I found out he was Taeyeon’s boyfriend!”

Her voice shook harder.

“I’d already fallen for him. But I was the one who had to step back, make room, hide. Pretend nothing had ever happened.”

“I tried so hard to forget him. I really did! And now you’re telling me we’re the same?”

“Do you know how it felt to watch them together? To see Taeyeon so happy and act as if I was fine, when it hurt that much?”

“You don’t know anything!”

“I do!” Yoon-a’s voice had grown hoarse too. “Eonni, I know.”

“When I stood behind that door today, listening to Taeyeon eonni, do you know what I was thinking?”

“I wished I’d never met him. I wished I hadn’t gotten sick.”

“I wished he’d only called to ask how I was, instead of coming all this way.”

“And I wondered…”

She stopped to draw a deep breath.

“Why did it have to be him?”

Tiffany said nothing.

“But after seeing you, I understand,” Yoon-a continued. “Why it’s him. Why it could only be him.”

Tiffany looked bewildered.

“We’re the same kind of person, eonni. We want success more than anyone. We’re ruthless about it. And we’re vain.”

“We fought our way through trainee selections and worked for years to become Girls’ Generation. People like us admire strength. We admire someone who can help us succeed!”

“No. That’s not it.” Tiffany shook her head instinctively. “Ji-ho and I are in love.”

“Then why did you need to wait when he was a trainee?”

“When you knew he couldn’t stay faithful, why did you put up with it after he gave you a villa?”

“Stop lying to yourself, eonni. If he’d stayed an ordinary trainee, would you really have dated him?”

“Even if he debuted and you got together, what if he never became successful? Would you have stayed for more than three years?”

Tiffany clenched her fists, her face draining of color.

Would she? If Ji-ho were just an ordinary trainee, would she have gone out with him?

Probably not. She might have wanted him, but perhaps it would have been something casual. Two or three years, then boredom and a breakup.

Wait. That wasn’t right.

A person’s appeal came from everything about them. Why was she letting herself get trapped in a childish argument about whether she loved him for his money?

That little schemer had almost talked her into it.

Yoon-a was younger and had gotten together with him later. She wanted Tiffany guilty and uncertain so she could climb over her!

Unbelievable.

Tiffany began rolling up her sleeves. Perhaps her dear dongsaeng needed a little education from her eonni.

The door clicked open.

They both turned. Ji-ho stood in the doorway.

Tiffany’s eyes were red, her cheeks still marked with tears. Yoon-a looked pale; at the sight of him, her gaze flickered and turned aside.

For a moment, none of them spoke.

Tiffany leaned forward to look past him. The corridor was empty.

“Why are you alone? You didn’t catch her?”

He was silent for two seconds before answering.

“No.”

Her shoulders sagged. Yoon-a seemed about to ask something, then merely pressed her lips together.

The silence was awkward.

Tiffany was still angry about the two of them. But after what Yoon-a had said, she no longer felt she had much right to speak.

And the way Ji-ho had used her full name earlier had unsettled her.

Leave him now? Not a chance.

She bit her lip, hesitated, then took out her phone.

“I’ll call Taeyeon.”

Both of them looked up. Tiffany avoided their eyes as she found the number, took a breath and switched on the speaker.

Tiffany calls Taeyeon · speakerphone in Room 308

The ringing seemed to go on forever. Then a muffled, rough voice answered, still thick from crying.

“Hello?”

“Taeyeon…”

Tiffany’s eyes reddened again. Her throat closed around the name. Several seconds passed before Taeyeon spoke.

“Mm. I’m here.”

“Where are you?”

Another silence.

“In the car. Going back to the hotel.”

Tiffany stared at the phone. Across from her, Ji-ho looked equally taken aback. The hotel? She was going back?

“Fany.”

“Mm?”

“Tell him…”

She stopped.

“Never mind. It’s nothing.”

“Taeyeon…”

“I’m hanging up.”

“Wait! Are you… are you all right?”

“All right? Fany, do you think I could be all right?”

Tiffany had no answer.

“But I will be.”

“Taeyeon…”

“I’ll go now. I’ll get back, have some sleep. Then I’ll be fine.”

The call ended.

Tiffany stared at the darkened screen, then slowly lowered her phone.

“She says she’s going back to the hotel.”

Ji-ho let out a long breath. At least she was safe.

“I’ll go back with you.”

“No.” Yoon-a came toward him, her expression serious. “Let me go first. I want to talk to Taeyeon eonni alone.”

“I know her. I think I can persuade her.”

He studied her for a moment before nodding. “All right.”

“I’ll call our manager eonni and ask her to arrange my discharge. Then I’ll get ready.”

“You’re leaving now?” Tiffany looked at her. “But you’re still sick…”

“Are you worried about me, eonni?”

“Who’s worried about you? I’m worried you’ll ruin tomorrow’s concert!”

“If you say so.” Yoon-a smiled. “I feel fine.”

“You know, eonni, perhaps you don’t know Ji-ho all that well. Even someone who came along as late as I did knows more.”

“Mwo? What’s that supposed to mean?”

Yoon-a only gave her a pleased little smile and went into the bathroom to wash and change.

Tiffany seized Ji-ho’s arm.

“Ya! What did she mean? What don’t I know about you? How could she possibly know you better than I do?”

He shrugged. Only someone as absentminded as Sica, or as thoroughly in love as Fany, could overlook the unusual things about being with him.

“Tell me! How can I lose to Yoon-a?”

“Where’s the fun in telling you? Take your time and work it out. Right now, I need your help.”

“With what?”

“Girls’ Generation sometimes invites special guests to perform at concerts, doesn’t it?”

“Of course. Back in 2010, Leeteuk oppa and Heechul oppa…”

She stopped, her eyes widening.

“Wait. You want to go onstage tomorrow?”

“Mm. I wrote ‘Some’ for Taeyeon nuna, but released it with Ji-eun. Nuna’s never quite gotten over that.”

“We’ve worked on an OST together, but she still wants that duet stage. I thought I’d give her one at the concert.”

“She’s still angry with you.”

“That’s why I need your help. Take me to the concert staff and let me arrange it first. Then we can surprise her.”

“Surprise her? More like give her a fright.” Tiffany pinched him.

“You’re awful! You’re counting on her being too embarrassed to refuse you in front of all those people, aren’t you?”

“Of course. She doesn’t want to see me now. Onstage, she won’t be able to turn me away.”

“Once we’ve sung together, half her anger will be gone. That’s step one. Then I’ll move on to step two. She won’t resist that.”

Watching him work so hard to coax Taeyeon softened none of Tiffany’s jealousy. She pouted.

“I’m not helping. We’re all your girlfriends. Yoon-a knows you better than I do, and you’re going to all this trouble for Taeyeon. What about me?”

Ji-ho slipped an arm around her waist and kissed her. Her resistance melted almost at once.

“You’re my darling, of course. Once this busy stretch is over, I’ll write you another song. Toward the end of the year, or next year.”

“Really?”

“When have I lied to you?”

She smiled. “Then… all right. I’ll help.”

“But what happens after the duet? How are you going to make it up to her? You sound awfully sure of yourself.”

“You’ll find out later.”

“Ya! Tell me!”

He glanced at the Papaya of Fuller Grace in his System inventory.

“No. Later.”

“Stingy.”

It wasn’t really a matter of favoring one woman over another. He only had two pieces. Surely the ones who needed them most should come first.

Yoon-a was the obvious choice. After her came Taeyeon and Ji-eun, who were much on a level in that particular respect.

Sica, Joo-hyun, Ji-yeon, Ha-ra, Sulli and Fany were already well provided for. If he obtained more later, he could think about them.

For now, he would use one to coax Taeyeon.

She wouldn’t be able to refuse it.

Her greatest concern had always been her height, and the Height-Reforging Milk had already addressed that. After height came… well, those little tangerines.

Not that he objected, of course. He was merely thinking of their future children. Surely they shouldn’t go hungry?

If he solved that worry for her and added a few sweet words, perhaps they could finally put the matter of Yoon-a behind them.

Once Taeyeon accepted Yoon-a, she would gradually get used to things. As for Sica, she might make a fuss, but he doubted it would last.

With that reassuring conclusion, he looked over the item’s description again.
