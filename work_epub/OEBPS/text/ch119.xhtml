Chapter One Hundred Nineteen

Running Man — The X-Man Special (II)

The directive phone was carried out on its little table. One by one they took the call — Haha first, then Jae-suk, Jong-kook, IU… each free to wear whatever face would best confuse the others afterward.

Last: Ji-ho. He lifted the receiver. A low male voice:

“Annyeonghaseyo, Ji-ho-ssi.”

“Ne, annyeonghaseyo.”

“You… are the X-Man…” The pause stretched long enough to raise his pulse. “That’s right. You are the X-Man.”

“Your directive: at final judgment, be on the same team as IU. Achieve that — and you win.”

His breath caught once — and he left the booth wearing a slow, meaningful smile for the camera.

Team Assignment

RED Yoo Jae-suk · Lee Kwang-soo · Ji-ho

YELLOW Song Ji-hyo · Gary · Ji Suk-jin

BLUE IU · Kim Jong-kook · Haha

guests split, women split, and the rookie assigned to the veteran shepherd — standard formation.

Team colors on, the squads boarded their sponsored cars — Jae-suk driving, Ji-ho shotgun, Kwang-soo folded into the back.

“Ji-ho-ssi — check the mission?”

“Sunbae-nim, just Ji-ho is fine.” He raised the card to the dash-cam and read: “Running Man versus X-Man. Proceed to Incheon Culture Park and find the Running Man flag.”

“Incheon Culture Park.” Jae-suk repeated it and pulled out. “Then I’ll drop the formalities too. Ji-ho-ya — call me Jae-suk hyung.”

“Ne, Jae-suk hyung.”

Kwang-soo hauled himself up between the seats.

“Ji-ho-ssi, let’s speak comfortably too—”

“NO.” Jae-suk cut him off with judicial severity.

“Ani— WHY? On what grounds?!”

“Kwang-soo-yah.” Glacial. “Ji-ho is EXTREMELY famous right now. Kindly do not bother him.”

Kwang-soo’s face cycled through six kinds of speechless. Ji-ho hastily spun in his seat and extended a hand to the back row.

“Animnida — Kwang-soo hyung, please, speak comfortably.”

Kwang-soo shook it with radiant, wounded gratitude.

The giraffe’s foolishness was pure persona — off-camera the man was sharp, high-EQ, and connected everywhere. A friend worth making.

Jae-suk, cruising, started humming the “Some” hook under his breath — the like-mine-but-not-mine bit — and Ji-ho and Kwang-soo joined in on the lover-not-lover line, three-part and shameless. Two birds: screen time secured, song promoted.

✦   ✦   ✦

Incheon Culture Park had two or three hundred fans waiting — Running Man at its imperial peak, IU being IU, and Ji-ho the season’s fever.

“AAAAH— Ji-ho OPPA~!”

“Oppa! Over here!”

He stepped down to a wall of support signs bearing his name and waved; the wave detonated a second, larger wave of sound.

The teams mustered on the lawn, and the PD raised the loudhailer.

Mission 1 · Dodgeball War
Each team defends the center circle in turn while the other two attack from outside. Protect your designated SURVIVOR. Survivor hit = elimination. Longest-surviving team wins.

no attack-time limit · both attacking teams throw simultaneously · draw for order

Jae-suk drew as team representative — and, famed cursed-hands that he was, promptly drew first defense.

“Ah, HYUNG!” Kwang-soo, betrayed. “Why didn’t you let JI-HO draw?! You KNOW your luck is garbage!”

“…Sorry.” Immediate, unconditional surrender. Then, pushing up his glasses: “HOWEVER. The pressing issue is selecting our Survivor.”

As the show’s registered coward, Jae-suk wanted the protected role with every fiber of his being. He assumed his great-detective face. “The Survivor needs agility… and a SMALL frame. For dodging. Therefore — hyung will sacrifice himself for this role.”

No objections. Then:

“Also — Kwang-soo-yah. You stand in FRONT.”

“HYUNG! Why me?! Ji-ho looks agile!”

“I can take the front, Kwang-soo hyung—” Ji-ho volunteered sweetly.

“Yah, Kwang-soo!” Jae-suk yanked Ji-ho back and turned grave. “This is STRATEGY. You’re the tallest — big target, draw the fire. Ji-ho stays back where his agility can PROTECT me. Right, Ji-ho?”

Ji-ho met the sincere (read: wholly self-interested) gaze and nodded along dutifully.

“…Ne. Jae-suk hyung is right.”

Kwang-soo trudged toward the sacrifice zone, muttering:

“I knew it… is being tall a CRIME now…”

Ji-ho’s private arithmetic: he was Red now, and needed to end on IU’s Blue. So — don’t get suspected, don’t let Red win, and ideally don’t let Blue win either. Save the swap for the final game. Meaning: play HARD, lose PRECISELY.

Round one: Blue and Yellow attacking. Kim Jong-kook took the ball, forearms flexing, a tiger sighting prey — and locked onto Kwang-soo, ignoring Jae-suk entirely. Kwang-soo met the tiger’s eyes and dove behind Jae-suk.

“YAH! LEE KWANG-SOO!! How could you—!!” — while frantically maneuvering behind Kwang-soo in turn.

Ji-ho, all visible loyalty, sprinted over to shield him.

“Hyung! Quick — behind me! I’ve got you!”

Jong-kook’s grin went feral and the first ball came in on a flat scream of air, shaving past Kwang-soo’s shirt. Then Gary, for Yellow, picked his moment—

Bad luck, it turns out, is a real and documented force. Kwang-soo, still rattled, took Gary’s throw squarely below the belt.

“AAGH—!” He folded, knees clamped, hit the grass — and then, for reasons known only to variety physics, began doing push-ups.

“Kwang-soo-yah! You okay?!” Gary arrived at a run, patting his back. “Sorry, sorry—”

Everyone else was in ruins. The variety gods feed whom they feed.

“Ji-ho-ya…” Kwang-soo, dragged from the field, grasped his hand like a dying soldier. “It’s… up to you now.”

“Kwang-soo hyung! Rest easy!” — delivered with heroic gravity over a strangled laugh.

Jong-kook’s second target: Jae-suk, now orbiting Ji-ho like a panicked moon. “Ji-ho-ya! Protect me! Protect HYUNG!” Ji-ho spread his long arms, mother-hen configuration — and when the throw came, timed his pivot and CAUGHT it clean out of the air.

“WAAAH!!!” The whole field. IU applauded despite herself: “So COOL!”

“WELL DONE, Ji-ho-ya!! You are DEFINITELY not the X-Man!!” Jae-suk clung to his arm, reborn.

Whereupon Gary, ball recovered, slung a low sneak-throw that tagged the celebrating Jae-suk square on the ankle.

“GARY-YAH!!! AMBUSH!!!” — from the ground, at full grievance.

Gary danced. Red team: eliminated at 59 seconds.

✦   ✦   ✦

Round two: Yellow defending, Ji-hyo the Survivor behind a Gary–Suk-jin wall. Jong-kook handed IU the ball for the experience; she surveyed the field at length, found no angle, and finally heaved it with noodle-armed conviction — soft, slow, and badly off-line.

Yellow braced. The ball sailed serenely wide of everyone —

bok.

— and beaned the just-returned Lee Kwang-soo in the head.

He stood blinking, hand on skull, looking from ball to IU.

“…MWO-YA? Me?? I’m on OFFENSE??”

“HAHAHAHA!!!”

IU crouched behind her own hands, shoulders heaving. Inexplicable. Indefensible. The man simply ATE luck for the artistic benefit of the broadcast.

“Yah! Ji-eun-ah! That’s INDISCRIMINATE fire! X-Man! You must be the X-Man!” Jae-suk, mid-throw, mouth never idle.

“Correct! Sowing chaos! Manufacturing internal strife! CLASSIC X-Man behavior!” Haha, pointing.

“ANI! I really just can’t aim!!” IU, hopping in place.

Time to bank goodwill with the future teammate.

“Ji-eun nuna’s athletic ability is — genuinely, catastrophically weak. I believe her completely.”

She shot him a grateful look, privately delighted. See? Falling for me on schedule. The suspicion dissolved; the assault on Yellow resumed. Ji-ho attacked with tremendous visible commitment and missed by scrupulous millimeters, every time. Gary threw his body in front of Ji-hyo, narrating: “Ji-hyo-yah — it’s Monday — my heart beats only for you—” Yellow held for 5 minutes 43 seconds before Jae-suk’s sneak-throw finally found her.

Round three: Blue defending, IU the Survivor. Kwang-soo’s first ball screamed toward Jong-kook’s head; the tiger evaded by a whisker and roared; Kwang-soo cowered — “Hyung, sorry! It curved! It CURVED!” — and the entire field, granted a once-a-year license to legally bombard Kim Jong-kook, exercised it with joy. Nobody threw at IU at all.

Which would not do. Ji-ho picked his window and put one precise ball off IU’s calf: Blue frozen at 3 minutes 32 seconds.

Mission 1 · Result

YELLOW WINS — 5:43  ·  BLUE 3:32  ·  RED 0:59

winning team earns one suspect-trade

Gary turned to Ji-hyo with maximum devotion: “Ji-hyo-yah. You decide.” Suk-jin objected — “Yah, what do you mean SHE decides, we deliberate—” — and was overruled in real time.

“Aigoo, oppa, deliberate WHAT? Ji-ho is obviously not the X-Man.” She beckoned. “Ji-ho-ya. Come here.”

Gary turned to Suk-jin, apologetic: “Suk-jin hyung, sorry — trade vests with Ji-ho and head over—” — whereupon Ji-hyo seized Suk-jin protectively and shoved GARY toward Red instead.

“Yah — you go.”

“Ji-hyo-yah. ME? You’re trading… ME?”

“Obviously. Now that there’s a new flame — what’s the old one still hanging around for?” She flapped both hands. “Go on. Quickly.”

Gary trudged toward Red through a gale of laughter, wearing the face of a man who could distinctly hear tragic ballad strings swelling somewhere in the middle distance.
