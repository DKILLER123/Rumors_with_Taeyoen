His hand at her waist tightened by a degree.

“Second.” He repeated it at low volume, with a distinct grievance in the timber. “Second. I’m quoting scripture at you off the cuff and I come in second. To a fan letter.”

She blinked once, sweetly, and the smile went deeper instead of answering.

Then she rose on her toes and closed the distance herself.

The scent arrived first, clean and faintly sweet, like orchid, like musk, like the inside of a word she wasn’t saying. He stopped asking. The river could have them.
