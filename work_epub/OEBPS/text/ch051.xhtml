Chapter Fifty-One

Life’s Candy

April 30, 2012 Jamsil — Ji-ho’s Apartment evaluation day · flawless weather

Ji-ho suited up in the Hip-Hop Kid loadout: oversized tee under a classic denim jacket, high-waist loose jeans, sneakers laced tight. The shades and the white Converse cap went into his bag — those were for the stage.

Outfit · Hip-Hop Kid Equipped

Completion: 70%

Effect: Live rap performance +1 tier. Projects unshakable confidence, transmits emotion, infects the audience — operating at 70%.

Then, from the system Inventory, he drew the Opportunity Card.

Item · Opportunity Card

Redeem one opportunity in a chosen domain: Romance · Money · Connections · Career.

Selection confirmed: CAREER. A new career opportunity will arrive shortly.

No hesitation — Career, confirmed. The card dissolved into a flare of light and was gone. He drew one long breath, stepped out the door, and set course for SM Entertainment.

✦   ✦   ✦

Nine a.m. sharp, he walked into the building — and was promptly detoured by a staffer into a small meeting room.

The door swung open. Sulli, seated inside, looked up at the sound — and the instant her eyes found him, the memory of a certain recent kiss arrived uninvited. Color climbed her cheeks; her gaze dove for the tabletop.

“Ji-ho-ssi.” The staffer across from her waved him in. “Come, sit.”

He shut the door quietly and took the seat beside Sulli.

“I’m Lee Seo-yeon, PR team.” The smile lasted exactly as long as the introduction. “Ji-ho — your internal commendation ceremony today has been upgraded. It’s now an SBS exclusive interview. After the photo session with the CEO, you and Sulli will sit for it together.”

Two printed A4 sheets landed on the table, one in front of each of them.

“Interview outline. You go on at nine-thirty — that gives you ninety minutes. Run through your answers together, flag anything you’re not comfortable addressing. I’ll be back in an hour and we’ll walk the full question list as a group.”

And she was gone, leaving the two of them blinking at each other across the sudden quiet.

“Pfft.”

“Haha—”

The shared laugh broke whatever awkwardness had been pooling. Sulli relaxed visibly, voice going soft.

“Oppa, what have you been busy with lately?”

“Prepping for the month-end evaluation.” A smile, returned. “You?”

She dug into her bag with sudden excitement, produced a driver’s license, and pressed it into his hands with tremendous ceremony, chin lifting in triumph.

“I used my whole rest period — and got my license!”

He flipped it open. The ID photo showed a Sulli grinning like sunrise. His congratulations came out entirely sincere.

“Well done, Sulli. Genuinely.”

“Heh. Actually — the original plan was to get it at twenty-two.”

“Oh? What changed?”

The grin softened into something more deliberate.

“Because loving yourself is the most important thing, right? I always used to write the things I wanted to do down as ‘wishes’ — someday things. But after what you said, I actually went back through the list… and so many of them were things I could just DO. Now. So I spent the time and got my license.”

“And? How did it feel, passing?”

“AMAZING, obviously!” Her eyes were throwing sparks now. “I was so excited the night I passed, I barely slept.” The brightness deepened as the picture unfolded in her. “From now on, between schedules — I can drive MYSELF somewhere. Find some quiet beach or a café, no manager tailing me, no panicking when a fan spots me. That kind of freedom… I can’t wait for it.”

And in those glittering eyes, at this exact moment, there was room for precisely one reflection — his. Oppa — I held this in for so long. I wanted to tell you in person, not over an app. I wanted you to SEE it: I’m changing. Little by little, I’m getting better.

“Good work, Sulli-yah.” His hand rose on its own and ruffled her hair, gently. “Life is bitter. The things you want to do — those are the candy. When you want one, eat one. Don’t hoard them for some perfect later. That’s the whole secret to a life that actually tastes like something.”

The gesture was far too intimate for the room they were in, and it froze her solid — the feather-light drag of fingers through her hair, the warmth of a palm at her crown. Her heart skipped one full beat; her ears ignited; the blush spread with total disregard for her dignity.

He seemed to register the shift in air pressure, withdrew the hand smoothly, and picked up the interview outline.

“Right — clock’s running. Let’s get through this thing.”

A small, unreasonable disappointment dropped through her — filed away in half a second, professional reflexes intact. She picked up her own sheet, and they worked.

Field Notes

SBS Interview Outline — selected questions:

For Ji-ho:

“When you overheard Choi’s conversation while working at the bar — what was your first thought? What made you decide to record, and then to intervene?” · “Facing Choi and his tattooed associate — did you hesitate?” · “Your courage has put a spotlight on you as an SM trainee. Can you share your current training progress — evaluation performance, your strongest areas?”

For Sulli:

“When you first saw the recording — shock, anger, something else? How have you processed it since?” · “SM arranged counseling and security support — how is your recovery, and is there anything you’d like to say to the fans who’ve stood by you?” · “Ji-ho’s intervention spared you the worst. Has a special trust or friendship formed between you? Any chance of future collaboration?”

A dozen-plus questions between them, none of them landmines — the PR team had clearly pre-negotiated every inch of ground with SBS. An hour later, Lee Seo-yeon returned on the dot; the three of them walked the list once more, cleared it, and moved to final prep.

✦   ✦   ✦

In SM’s grand conference room, Ji-ho held up a presentation placard — Special Courage Commendation · ₩50,000,000 — with Kim Young-min flanking him on the left and Sulli on the right, all three smiling on cue as the SBS cameras strobed the moment into permanence.

Photos wrapped quickly.

“Reporter Choi — today’s interview is in your capable hands.”

“Kim sajang-nim, you flatter me. My department head sends his regards — and thank you for giving SBS the exclusive.”

“Pass my greetings back to Director Kim. I’ve got matters waiting, so I’ll excuse myself here.”

“Of course, of course.”

The CEO departed; Ji-ho and Sulli bowed him out in unison.

“Sajang-nim, safe travels.”

“Ji-ho-ssi, Sulli-ssi — please, have a seat. Let’s begin.”
