Chapter Two Hundred Two

The Taste of Taeyeon’s Lip Balm

June 4, 2013 · early evening The villa — living room, and one locked bathroom strawberry sweetness that tastes bitter — and a secret traded for a kiss

Tiffany’s mouth held its smile by mechanical force. On her lap, out of sight, her nails had pressed four deep crescents into the soft of her palm. The sting was the only thing keeping the face composed — the only thing holding back the sour tide climbing her throat.

Listening to Yoon-a’s kitchen report, her heart had been squeezed by an invisible fist until breathing required planning.

Shortly, the couple returned bearing a tray of seven fragrant cups. Taeyeon’s cheeks still carried their blush; her lips were fuller and redder than they’d been ten minutes ago — cherry-ripe, incriminating.

“Aigoo~ our Kim-leader went to brew coffee and came back with swollen LIPS?”

Yuri fired first, phone down, grin slanted.

“Ah~ the coffee must have been too hot. Right, Taeyeon-ssi?” Sunny picked it up in full drama-recap cadence.

“You two — SHUT UP!”

Taeyeon slammed cups down in front of them with lethal intent, visibly wishing coffee could be administered as a gag.

He just laughed and distributed the rest. When he handed Tiffany hers, their fingertips grazed — one spark down the wire — and she dropped her eyes fast, murmuring thanks.

He caught it anyway: the effortful smile, the light going briefly out. He sighed somewhere internal, and moved on, because there was nothing else to be done — not here, not now.

He took the single armchair; Taeyeon flowed into the space against his side like water finding its level.

The room warmed back up. Tour talk: the world tour opening in days, the calendar going vertical after. His side: film promotion starting tomorrow — variety tapings, theater tours, the full sprint.

At the mention of the film, he cleared his throat and sat up straighter, arm still around Taeyeon’s waist.

“So — nuna-deul.” An artfully sheepish smile. “The film opens around July. When the time comes — a mention on SNS, or a word on a broadcast—”

“I refuse.”

Yoon-a cut him off instantly, arms crossed, a beautiful counterfeit smile on her face, voice at wind-chill.

“I never do promotion for people who are STINGY and FORGETFUL. Terribly sorry, Ji-ho-ssi.”

He absorbed the hit and rubbed his forehead. “Mianhae, Yoon-a nuna. The birthday — I’m genuinely, deeply sorry. That stretch of days I was tied up in something and running blind. It was never on purpose.”

“Busy?” She looked him up and down with open suspicion. “You’re not on comeback. Film promo starts TOMORROW. What exactly were you busy with before that?”

And at that question, two other sets of ears in the room quietly rose to attention. Taeyeon’s. Tiffany’s. It was true — these weeks they’d been buried in rehearsal, but his line had been oddly textured lately: calls answered instantly some days, hours-late others. What WAS he busy with?

He smiled and kept it soft-focus. “Preparing something — important to me. There’s no certainty in it yet. The moment it firms up, you’ll all be the first to know.”

Tiffany’s fingertips tightened on her cup. Something important — the 460 billion?

Jessica raised her coffee and hid behind the rim. The 460 billion. Is he moving? The curiosity itched like a wool sweater on bare skin — and there was no possible way to scratch it in this room. Agony, in cashmere.

“All mysterious and secretive—” Taeyeon muttered, unbothered, and rolled the conversation onward to tour stories.

A while later, Tiffany rose abruptly.

“Excuse me — the restroom. Ji— Ji-ho. Could you tell me where it is?”

“End of that hallway. I’ll show you.”

He stood and led the way, entirely natural. Taeyeon suspected nothing — waved after her, smiling. “Come back quick~”

Down the hallway, one behind the other. At the bathroom door he had barely lifted his hand to gesture — here it is — when her hand clamped his wrist, hauled him bodily inside, and the lock snapped shut behind them.

In the next second his back hit cold tile, and she was up on her toes, kissing him — hard, hot, urgent, with no technique at all. Just pressure: weeks of swallowed grievance, jealousy, and want, all discharged at once. Her hands fisted in his shirtfront; her body trembled minutely against his.

Since walking into this house she had watched Taeyeon hold his arm like breathing. Listened to Yoon-a narrate the kitchen. Every single minute had been a slow burn under the skin. This man was HERS too — and her role tonight was set dressing: sit quietly, smile at intervals, watch her own intimacy performed by someone else.

And between his lips and hers, unmistakable, she tasted it: strawberry. Taeyeon’s lip balm. Sweet — and, secondhand, it went down like something scorched.

Bitter all the way to the heart.

The tears arrived without clearance. She broke back half an inch, breathing fast and hot, voice cracking.

“Tell me— please just tell me. What are you actually busy with?”

“Ji-ho — I’m begging you. Give me one thing— even one small thing that’s MINE and not hers— one place where I’ve won against Taeyeon— I can’t keep doing this—”

She tipped her face up. The eyes that curved into crescents for a living were flooded, wrecked, pleading.

His chest cramped hard.

He raised a hand and wiped the tear-track from her cheek with his thumb. Held her gaze a few seconds. Then, very low:

“I’m acquiring LOEN.”

Her pupils blew wide; her breath stopped; a cry was already halfway up her throat—

He bent and sealed his mouth over hers, swallowing the sound whole. This kiss ran long and gentle, and by degrees the shaking under his hands went still.

When he finally released her:

“It’s in motion. I’d put it at seventy percent now.”

“And right now — you’re the ONLY one I’ve told, Fany.”

He looked into her blurred, shining eyes and set each word down like a signature.

“Trust me. The future you want — I can build it.”

Acquiring LOEN. Her mind whited out around the words, then re-lit in a cascade. If he pulled that off— LOEN’s market cap north of five hundred billion won. Overnight he’d stand past even Lee Soo-man — teacher Lee’s whole estimated worth ran around 180 billion — and not as an artist, as an OWNER. The industry map redrawn. Lee Soo-man was sixty-one this year. Ji-ho was twenty.

Adoration, love, that face — and now a secret so large even TAEYEON didn’t hold it. HER secret. Hers alone. The feelings hit her in one breaking wave — weak knees, hammering pulse, and a fierce, private, guilty joy she couldn’t have named in either of her languages.

She rose on her toes and kissed him again — and this time there was nothing broken in it at all.

Some minutes later, two people made careful repairs at the mirror — hair smoothed, clothing squared, one lipstick reapplied with a steady hand. Inspection passed.

They returned to a living room mid-laughter: Yuri was butchering a joke, and the room was down for the count anyway.

“What took so long?” Taeyeon asked, entirely without suspicion, patting the seat beside her.

“Ani — that toilet of his. It’s nothing like the dorm’s.”

“It’s the SMART kind.”

“Extremely difficult to operate.”

Tiffany delivered the complaint with full hand choreography, laughing. Taeyeon looped an arm around her shoulders, instantly companionable. “The first time really is confusing. Not intelligent AT ALL, that thing.”

He resettled into the armchair and lifted his coffee, gaze moving calm and level across the bright, laughing room. Coffee aroma. Six perfumes. Taeyeon’s strawberry balm. Tiffany’s rose lipstick.

Across the room, Yoon-a’s eyes narrowed one imperceptible degree.

Fany eonni’s lips looked… slightly swollen?
