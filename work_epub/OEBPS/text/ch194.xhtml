Chapter One Hundred Ninety-Four

Face at Full Value

The same night → the days after The private room → two apartments → the studio the system settles accounts — and two women lose sleep over one face

The half-transparent interface unfolded across his vision, and her file rendered in the system’s elegant type:

⟨ DING ⟩ Acquired: Celebration Package ×1 · Limited Outfit: Lovers’ Whisper · Trait copied to player: EYE-SMILE MOONLIGHT

Lovers’ Whisper — silk slip or plain-toned intimates; an atmosphere of warm domesticity or quiet night. Attached effect, Hearts Aligned: one glance, one gesture, and each knows the other’s need — body and soul in register; endurance and recovery raised for both.

His breath caught slightly on the outfit’s fine print. The system’s limited wardrobe always carried effects. This one read — potent.

And the heavier notice was still queued behind it:

DREAM LIFE — FACE VALUE: MAXIMUM

⟨ DING ⟩ Face has reached full value: 10. Passive skill awarded: ETERNAL PRIME.

COREYour appearance is locked at its absolute peak for 20 years. Every future appearance-related bonus converts to +5 years of lock time. No stacking limit.

“When the face reaches its summit, the bloom becomes permanent. Years shall not touch it; wounds shall not mark it; every grace hereafter only lengthens the noon.”

His pupils tightened as he read it twice. From tonight forward, the face held — indefinitely extendable. Every appearance bonus the system ever paid out again would buy five more years of noon.

DREAM LIFE — STATUS · CHARM (UPDATED)
CHARM72

Face 10 · Physique 8 · Voice 9 · Presence 7 · Experience 7 · Communication 6 · Confidence 9 · Learning 7 · Wealth 2 (UNDISCLOSED — +8 to informed parties) · Fame 7

⟨ DING ⟩ Charm ≥ 70 — Charm Grand Package acquired.

He drew one long breath and opened the celebration package first.

Celebration Package · AFFINITY AURA (LV.1)

EFFECTNatural Rapport — on any first contact, the other party instinctively reads you as warm, agreeable, close-feeling. Hardened journalists, exacting partners, total strangers: the communication threshold drops, goodwill rises, first responses skew positive.

Quietly excellent. In an industry that ran half on faces and half on relationships, “natural rapport” was a standing discount on every negotiation he’d ever have.

The prompt tone faded — and the change arrived in the body at once. Not a renovation; a refinement. As the description had it: the dust of years rinsed away, the original fullest light let through.

✦   ✦   ✦

Tiffany raised her eyes — and stalled completely.

It was the same face. Same brows, same nose, same clean-cut mouth. And something had shifted in the substrate. The skin held the warm light differently — poreless, lit from within. The lines of the eyes and brow sat a degree deeper, truer, as if a master had made one final pass. And the eyes themselves—

The amber had opened into something you could fall into. Galaxy-depth. Looking too long felt like a risk with no insurance.

Her heart broke into a run.

Is this what they call the beloved’s filter? No — she’d seen handsome. The industry MANUFACTURED handsome. This was — implausible.

“What is it?” He’d caught her staring, voice gentle — and even the voice seemed to have dropped a half-step into something darker and finer.

“You—” Her mouth opened; heat climbed her face without permission. “—look so… good.”

It embarrassed her the instant it landed. It was also simply true. The man in front of her was handsome past processing — handsome enough to derail pulse and policy at once. She kissed him again.

This one ran hotter — both arms around his neck, up on her toes, very nearly hanging from him, kissing like a person confirming a rumor too good to trust: that this impossibly finished creature was hers.

He felt the tremor in her, the fever, the — surrender. When they came apart her lips were wine-bright and her eyes had gone soft and shining.

“How are you — MORE handsome than this afternoon?” she mumbled, dazed.

He smiled — and under Eye-Smile Moonlight the smile came out frankly weaponized. “I’m not. Maybe your eyes changed.”

Which was, technically, half true.

“…Maybe.” She muttered it — and looked again anyway. And thought, with perfect clarity and no resistance left: if this is a sin, fine. She’d sign.

✦   ✦   ✦

He called Ryu Dong-hwan to drive.

The ride to the dorm ran silent in the back seat, Seoul’s neon pulling long ribbons across the glass. She leaned at the window with her thoughts in full riot.

On one side: the tribunal. Taeyeon was her best friend — trainee years, Black Ocean, every trench of it, shared. Betraying that made her — she couldn’t finish the sentence.

On the other: the tide. He was too complete. Talent, tenderness — now add a face that weakened knees and four hundred sixty billion won. And more than any of it: the way he looked at her; the way he’d said “I can’t let you go” like a fact of physics.

So she prayed the only prayer left: if hell is the price, at least let the sentence run concurrent — with him.

He felt the war in the seat beside him — the tremor in her fingers, the eyes that wouldn’t land — and let her fight it in peace.

The car stopped below the dorm. She didn’t move.

He checked: Star Counter-Surveillance silent, no lenses anywhere. He looked at the driver’s seat. “Hyung. Leave me the keys.”

“Don’t need me to run you home after?”

“No. Thanks. Long night — go rest.”

“Keep the courtesies. Pad my year-end bonus instead.”

“Haha. Hyung, relax. This year it might be more than a bonus.”

Dong-hwan didn’t parse it, but he knew from experience the man was generous, and left smiling.

A steady hand, that hyung — knew every line and never crossed one. When LOEN landed, there’d be a real post waiting for him.

Tiffany got out, and stalled again at the sight of his profile under the dim streetlight.

“Ji-ho.”

“Mm?”

“Tonight — I said yes. But until I’ve thought it all the way through, we — hold at this line. Don’t push me. Give me a little time. Okay?”

He turned, eyes unhurried and deep. “I can. The question is — can YOU?”

She bit her lip.

She was not remotely sure. Looking at the face alone made her want to close the distance. The memory of the kiss was already running hot laps under her skin.

“I’ll — try.”

He laughed, reached out, and ruffled her hair — an intimacy so casual it skipped her heart sideways. She bolted for the entrance.

“Good night, Tiffany nuna,” came the voice behind her, warm and shameless. “Send Taeyeon nuna down for me.”

“Shibal— you absolute BASTARD!”

The elevator doors ate her fury.

Unbelievable. UNBELIEVABLE. He wants ME to fetch HER. Bastard bastard BASTARD—

In the elevator mirror she performed damage control: lips faintly swollen, cheeks pink, eyes shifty. A full page of evidence.

When she pushed open the dorm door, the first person she saw was, of course, Taeyeon — cross-legged on the rug, “Shallow” lyric book open on her lap. Her head came up at the latch.

“Fany? You’re back. Did the session go well?”

“F-fine. It went fine.”

Natural. Sound natural. Do not make eye contact.

“Why’s your face so red? Were you drinking?”

“A little — red wine. He said — to celebrate the session going well.”

Taeyeon nodded and went back to the lyrics. Three seconds later the head came up again, eyes carrying a new gram of doubt. “Fany. Are you — okay? You seem strange.”

“Fine! Just tired!” The retreat to her room was very nearly a sprint. “Shower! Going to shower!”

“Oh — right. He’s waiting for you downstairs.”

Door shut. Back against it. One long, long exhale.

Too close.

In the living room, Taeyeon frowned at the closed door. She was sensitive to exactly one category of thing above all others: the weather inside people she loved. And Fany tonight had been all wrong — the flush, the fugitive eyes, the clipped answers, that unmistakable static of guilt. And something odd in her voice on his name.

She shook her head hard, twice.

Impossible. Fany KNOWS about us. Fany would never. I’m overthinking. I’m ALWAYS overthinking.

She looked back down at the lyric book and processed none of it. His face had wandered into the space between her and the page.

Weird. Why is my heart pounding just THINKING about him today?

She packed her things, changed shoes, and went down with a light step, scanned the street, and darted into his car like a gust of wind. The driver’s seat was empty; he leaned forward from the back.

“Nuna — you drive. I’ve had a little wine.”

She caught the faint edge of alcohol, wrinkled her nose, and climbed over to the wheel. “You went drinking with Fany?”

“The session was punishing — retake after retake. Dinner was the least I owed her. A little red wine against the pressure.”

“Oh.”

The tone was seamless. She started the car and thought no more about it.

✦   ✦   ✦

Ten p.m., back at the apartment — he went up first; two together made too big a target. She parked (not quickly; parking was not among her superpowers) and came in just as he stepped out of the bathroom: loose sleep clothes, hair still dripping, water tracking down from the ends.

And she stopped dead in the hallway.

Under the lamp, his skin seemed faintly self-lit. The wet hair raked back off the full clean line of his forehead. And when the eyes came up to hers — something moved in them like a slow river of stars.

“Nuna? Go shower.” He crossed toward her, smiling.

Her mouth opened. No audio.

Same person. Same bed as yesterday. So why is the pull — three times the dose?

“What’s wrong?” A hand waved gently in front of her eyes. “Nuna?”

“Ah— n-nothing. Just—” scarlet now, “—you look ridiculously good today.”

She regretted it instantly. Great. Now I sound like a fansite.

He smiled — Eternal Prime running at full spec — and said, simply: “Because I missed you.”

One plain sentence. That face. Her knees performed a brief systems failure, and then she was across the hallway and into him, up on her toes, kissing him — urgent, hungry, with an edge of possession she didn’t bother examining. Her arms locked around his neck; the shower could wait; everything could wait.

The rest of the night belongs to the two of them.

Moonlight came through the curtain gap and lay across the room. Somewhere in the warm blur of it she thought, drowsy and shameless: if this is a dream, let it never end.

✦   ✦   ✦

Across the city, in the dorm, Tiffany lay staring at her ceiling.

His face. His mouth. His hands. His voice. And four hundred sixty billion won.

She rolled over and buried her face in the pillow.

“Shibal—”

Muffled, and aimed at no one she could name — him, or herself, or the ceiling.

And the corner of her mouth, entirely without authorization, curved upward — the signature eye-smile bending into crescents in the dark.

Two women lost sleep that night over the same man. The man himself, holding the soft weight in his arms, crossed quietly into a new phase of the game.

Face: eternal. Fortune: staggering. Charm: capped and climbing.

“Ji-ho-ya. I love you.”

“I know.”

“No — you don’t know. Without you… I’d die.”

“I’ll be right here. Nuna. Taeyeon. My soft one.”

“My main-vocal-nim.”

She slept mid-smile.

✦   ✦   ✦

The recording days after that ran fast and clean: Taeyeon mornings, Tiffany afternoons. Two best friends; two entirely different climates. Taeyeon — cream-soft with a queen’s spine underneath, sweet without cloying. Tiffany — heat and brightness, provocation without vulgarity. He moved between the two weathers with a composure he had absolutely not earned, and knew it, and enjoyed it anyway.

When the wealth went public — when LOEN sat in his pocket — when the position was past assault: Ji-eun. Yoon-a. Jiyeon. Beauty like jade, and he wanted more of it. He’d stopped pretending otherwise.

Three days, and both songs were in the can. He shipped the masters to Kwak Jae-yong for the edit; Taeyeon and Tiffany folded back into Girls’ Generation’s tour machine. Rehearsals ate Taeyeon whole — she didn’t come back to the apartment even once, leaving him a full day of empty rooms.

No big sister around — fine; there was a little sister to check on. He’d been meaning to see Joo-hyun anyway, and to talk through the shape of her future—

The phone rang. Unexpected name.

Voice Call · Jessica

“Sica nuna. What’s the occasion?”

“Ji-ho. Are you free tonight? I’d like to buy you dinner.”

“Dinner?”

“Mm. There are some things I want to ask you. Properly. If you have the time.”

He weighed it a moment. He’d been meaning to take Jessica’s temperature anyway — Taeyeon prized Girls’ Generation above almost everything, and he had no intention of letting the team become eight on his watch.

“Sure. Text me the time and place. I’ll be punctual.”
