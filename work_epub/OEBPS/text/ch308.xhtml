Chapter Three Hundred Eight

A Born Idol

Saturday, October 5, 2013 · before rehearsal Singapore · Mount Elizabeth Novena Hospital

“Then it’s settled, seonsaengnim.”

Ji-ho listened to Lee Soo-man’s answer.

“Yes. I’ll have LOEN’s public-relations department coordinate the publicity. Ne. Goodbye, seonsaengnim.”

He ended the call.

Yoon-a had finished getting ready. Her white blouse had puff sleeves and a flowing ribbon at the neck; fitted jeans emphasized her long, slender figure.

With her hair gathered into a neat ponytail, she looked elegant and effortlessly expensive. She only had to stand there to become Girls’ Generation’s visual center again.

“Lee Soo-man seonsaengnim agreed to have you as a special guest?” she asked.

Ji-ho smiled. “I haven’t performed live since buying LOEN. Appearing as a mystery guest at your concert will get people talking.”

“We could have the headlines to ourselves for a while. It’s good for Girls’ Generation and good for my upcoming album. Why would seonsaengnim turn that down?”

“There’ll certainly be headlines.” Yoon-a’s gaze shifted. “All about you and Taeyeon eonni.”

She hesitated, then lowered her voice.

“Are you… going to make your relationship public?”

Tiffany looked up too. Once he made that declaration, a great many things would change.

Ji-ho avoided a direct answer.

“We’re young. Our careers come first.”

Yoon-a and Tiffany both let out a quiet breath.

“Let’s go.”

The manager had completed the discharge paperwork. The three of them got into the van and left the hospital.

Earlier, when Taeyeon returned to the hotel room, Jessica was putting on her makeup.

“Taeyeon? Why are you alone? Where are Fany and Yoon-a?”

Taeyeon stared for a moment before gathering herself.

“Oh. Today’s the final rehearsal, isn’t it? Yoon-a still needed her discharge checks. Fany stayed with her, so I came back first.”

“I see.” Jessica went back to her lipstick, accepting the explanation without suspicion. “Then we’d better get going.”

The van rolled smoothly through the city. Tiffany glanced at her phone.

“Yuri says they’re already at the Indoor Stadium.”

Ji-ho considered it. “Then let’s go straight there. Yoon-a, you join the others for rehearsal. Fany, take me to meet the director first.”

“All right.”

Singapore Indoor Stadium was one of Asia’s great concert venues. Its broad, sloping roof suggested an inverted cone, echoing the lines of an Asian temple.

Inside, no columns interrupted the space. The venue covered more than fifty thousand square meters, rising to forty-seven meters at its highest point.

The stage could expand to nearly three thousand square meters. An enormous four-sided Sony display hung overhead. It certainly looked the part.

Yoon-a went to rejoin the group as soon as they arrived. Tiffany led Ji-ho into a backstage lounge.

“I’ll get the director.”

When she had gone, he finally had a moment to examine Yoon-a’s rewards. He hadn’t had the chance since the night before.

He opened the Celebration Package.

The reward was called A Scoundrel’s Special Touch.

Kisses, touch, intimate understanding: different women have different preferences. You recognize those preferences and respond with exceptional finesse.

Exactly what he should have expected from Yoon-a. Useful rewards.

Born Idol’s two appearance points had lengthened Eternal Prime. Camera’s Darling would help him in front of a lens, whether onstage or filming.

As for A Scoundrel’s Special Touch…

Completely unnecessary. His performance was already excellent.

Although, now that he thought about it, he did tend to follow his own preferences. With Joo-hyun, it was her curves. With Ji-yeon, those long legs…

He cleared his throat.

Perhaps the reward wasn’t so bad after all.

A knock sounded at the door.

“Come in.”

Tiffany entered with the director, a man in his forties. He came quickly toward Ji-ho and bowed.

“Hoejang-nim, annyeong haseyo. I’m Lee Kang-jun, the stage director.”

Ji-ho rose with a smile and offered his hand. “Director Lee. Good to meet you.”

Lee Kang-jun supported his right wrist with his left hand and bent slightly as they shook.

“Ne, Hoejang-nim. It’s an honor.”

“No need to be so formal.” Ji-ho released his hand. “Please, sit. Let’s talk.”

“I won’t stay,” Tiffany said. “I should get back to rehearsal.”

“All right. Go on.”

She gave him a sweet smile and left.

Ji-ho turned to Lee Kang-jun. “How is tomorrow night’s show arranged?”

The director composed himself and considered his answer.

“We have twenty-five songs: twenty-two in the main set, plus three for the encore. There aren’t any individual solo stages for the members.”

“I thought we could put your collaboration with Taeyeon at the fourteenth song, around the eighty-minute mark. What do you think?”

There was a reason concert guests often appeared in the later middle of a show, roughly sixty to seventy percent of the way through.

For a two-hour concert, that meant a broad window of about seventy to ninety minutes, when the audience was ready for the next high point.

The earlier ballads and group stages built the atmosphere. Just as the crowd grew eager for a surprise, the guest arrived and pushed the excitement higher.

One to three songs, a little interaction, then the stage returned to the main artists. The energy could settle into the closing stretch before the encore.

For someone of Ji-ho’s standing, Lee Kang-jun’s suggestion made sense.

Ji-ho thought it over. Taeyeon was still angry with him.

If he appeared midway through and upset her, she would have to get through the rest of the show afterward. That could cause trouble.

He chose the other common option.

“Put my appearance in the final section before the encore.”

“I’ll sing one song myself, then a duet with Taeyeon. After that, one song with Girls’ Generation.”

“The group can close with the encore.”

A late guest appearance would be a final surprise, leading directly into the encore. Even if Taeyeon’s emotions wavered, he reckoned it would disturb less of the show.

Lee Kang-jun nodded. “Ne. I’ll adjust it right away.”

“Which songs would you like to sing?”

“‘Eyes, Nose, Lips’ for the solo. ‘Some’ with Taeyeon.”

Ji-ho considered the last choice.

“And for the group number… ‘Into the New World.’”
