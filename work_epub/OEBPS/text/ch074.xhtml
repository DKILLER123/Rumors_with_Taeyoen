Chapter Seventy-Four

Sulli’s Call

Downstairs, the fan-meeting production meeting was in session.

“Yoon-a — after the dance segment we draw six to eight fans for stage photos; you’ll present signed Polaroids. Then free distribution of your character cookies and drinks, with the MD table selling photobooks, doll keychains, cushions, hand fans. All merchandise proceeds donated to charity in your name.”

She took her birthday fan meeting seriously — she’d shaped half the plan herself in prep — and after listening closely, offered a few final adjustments and gaveled the meeting closed.

Off-duty at LAST~

“Yoon-a — a birthday present came for you.”

Her manager entered carrying an elegant gift box.

“Eonni? From who?”

“Ji-ho. Dropped it off an hour ago — you were in the meeting, so I signed for it.” The manager was visibly delighted with the errand. “First time I’ve seen him in person. He has one of those faces that LOOKS talented — no wonder he could write something like ‘Some’!”

“Mwoya—” Yoon-a laughed, accepting the box. “Eonni, you haven’t even HEARD it. How would you know it’s good?”

“In-jun heard it! He went with Producer Choi to see him. He’s basically a fan now — keeps telling me all week that if Ji-ho debuts, he explodes. Guaranteed.”

Yoon-a caught the important part instantly, smile going sly.

“Eonni. You and Producer In-jun…?”

“We’re… still at the getting-to-know-each-other stage.” The manager went pink.

“REALLY? Chukahae, eonni!” Genuine delight — followed immediately by genuine mischief. “No WONDER Producer In-jun loves ‘Some’ so much. A man in the exact ambiguous stage himself~”

“Mm. He says it sings his actual heart.” The manager nodded, then added: “Oh — and the female vocal on the demo? That’s Taeyeon.”

A half-beat pause. Then a smile.

“I know.”

“You KNOW?”

“Mm.”

No further explanation offered. She bent to the box instead: a premium snack assortment — and her eyes lit despite herself. The boy can PICK a gift. On top, a small card:

Gift card · attached

Nuna — happy birthday. Also, for the record: my birthday is January 21st.

At 5:30 that evening, dance practice done, Ji-ho retrieved his phone from the locker — two missed calls, both from Sulli. He found a quiet corner outside the cafeteria and called back.

Outgoing call · Sulli

“Oppa! Practice over?”

“Mm, just finished. How’s the album coming?”

“All DONE — official release June 10th! The title track is so good, oppa. You have to support us~”

“Support? I’ll be cheering for you.”

“OPPA! Who supports with CHEERING?!”

“Then what counts as support?”

“Actual PURCHASES, obviously! It’s our second mini album in three whole years since debut. With OUR relationship — oppa won’t even support me a little?”

“But don’t artists give albums away? With OUR relationship — you won’t even gift me one?”

“Of course I’m gifting you one! A special collector’s edition — signed photo, hardcover photobook, postcards, poster, limited photocard—”

“That good? Then if you’re giving me the deluxe version anyway… why exactly am I also buying one? Same thing, no?”

“It is NOT the same!! The one I give you is MY heart! The one you buy is YOUR heart! How can that be the same—”

“Hahahaha— okay, okay. I surrender. Of course I’m buying Sulli’s album.”

“…Oppa. You teased me ON PURPOSE. So hateful~”

Then she went quiet — ear pressed to the phone, listening to him laugh — and ended up laughing along with him. When it faded, a three-second pause, and then, carefully:

Call · continued

“Oppa… are you resting this weekend?”

“I’d planned to keep practicing. But if you’re free — let me take you to dinner. If I eat one more cafeteria meal, something in me dies.”

“Heehee — PERFECT. Album prep’s finally done and I have a few days off. I want to buy the car this weekend — otherwise the album drops on the 10th, I’m on a drama set by month’s end, and it gets pushed AGAIN for months.”

“Deal. I’ll escort you to the dealership, then dinner’s on me.”

“You’re helping me buy a car — dinner is MINE. The meal you owe me stays owed. Next time!”

“Fine, fine. So — which car?”

“BMW Z4! I saw it ONCE and it was over for me.”

“Got it. I’ll study the specs this week.”

“Thank you, oppa! Weekend, then — manager eonni’s yelling for me. Bye bye~”

He pocketed the phone, smiling faintly. A convertible roadster, chosen on sight. Underneath all those years of trained obedience, the real Sulli had always been this: loud-hearted, sun-natured, rebellious and utterly candid. The pleaser’s shell was cracking — and what was emerging was someone worth meeting.
