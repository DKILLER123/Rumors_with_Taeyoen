Chapter Eighty-Eight

A Sense of Crisis

No one was happier than Wendy — fully, hopelessly converted into Ji-ho’s number-one fangirl by now. The moment the rumor reached her she called him, and he didn’t hide it from her; he only asked her to keep it quiet.

So… in his heart, I’m special.

She promised, floating somewhere three feet above the floor, heart doing small ridiculous cartwheels.

Irene heard the same news — and went silent.

The same nineteen. The same “high-aged” trainee. The same last-one-out-of-the-practice-room lights.

She had admired his effort. Seen her own reflection in it. Her heart had jumped once when he played the hero on that motorcycle; jumped again at the monthly evaluation when his talent filled the whole room; and every midnight she coached his dancing, she had quietly countersigned his stubbornness with her own.

Now he had climbed to the pre-debut team. He was about to debut. And her?

The company wouldn’t launch a new girl group for at least two more years — and whether she’d even make the lineup was written nowhere.

What good is a perfect evaluation record? What good is being the legendary trainee?

Does effort really lose to talent — every single time?

Her fists tightened.

Or maybe I haven’t given enough yet. Fine. Starting today — more.

✦   ✦   ✦

The noise outside had nothing to do with Ji-ho. Promotion to the pre-debut team came with production-room privileges, and he put them to immediate, ruthless use — reconstructing, from memory, the songs that had once conquered the charts of his first life.

Priority went to next year’s arsenal: EXO’s “Growl.” Girls’ Generation’s “I Got a Boy.” “My Destiny,” the love theme from My Love from the Star.

Practice. Write. Message Taeyeon, Tiffany, Sulli.

The surprise was Jiyeon — total silence, as if that night had been a beautiful accident and nothing more.

Doesn’t care? Or can’t face it?

He had no way of knowing the truth: Jiyeon had blacked out completely, and remembered nothing at all.

He didn’t chase. He was still a trainee — what exactly would showing up accomplish?

Instead he transcribed harder. Not just the peninsula’s future hits — the American charts too, the songs he’d grown up hearing bleed through apartment walls in Koreatown. If they landed the way they had in his last life, the publishing alone would let him retire flat on his back.

He’d also quietly surveyed the rest of the industry. Small agencies: seductive splits, starving resources. Big agencies: rich resources, carnivorous contracts.

So until SM formally broke with him, he wouldn’t knock on other doors. Because whatever else was true, SM’s operating machine remained the strongest on the peninsula.

June 8, 2012 · 10:17 PM SM Entertainment — Production Room two intruders, zero footsteps

Two tiptoeing silhouettes eased the production-room door open and slipped inside.

Ji-ho was buried in the work, brow faintly creased, whole body folded into his own world — tonight was upload night. The demos for “Growl” and “I Got a Boy” were going into the company song library.

SM’s two biggest weapons of next year — claimed in advance.

Taeyeon stood in the doorway with a half-month’s worth of missing him boiling behind her eyes. Ji-ho. I missed you so much.

Two weeks and change apart, and it had stretched like years.

She bit her lip, glanced at Tiffany beside her, pressed the surge back down — and then her eyes did a slow, wicked orbit as the mischief woke up. A finger to her lips. A point at his back. Tiffany nodded, co-conspirator instantly.

Taeyeon rose onto her toes like a small secret agent on final approach, advancing step by silent step.

SM Song Library · Upload Console
SAVE PROJECT✔ COMPLETE
LIBRARY LOGIN — SONG J.✔ AUTHENTICATED
UPLOAD · “GROWL” (DEMO)✔ 100%
UPLOAD · “I GOT A BOY” (DEMO)✔ 100%

registration timestamped — claimed.

Done. Ji-ho exhaled long and slow, stretching luxuriously back in the chair.

“Ahh~ finally finished!”

One step behind him, Taeyeon coiled — sprang the last little hop — slapped both hands down on his shoulders and yelled at the top of her lungs:

“HEY!”

Bright, crisp, and absolutely deafening in the silent room.

Ji-ho jackknifed forward with a full-body jolt, nearly cracking his head on the desk edge. He whipped around, face blanched for one heartbeat — and the instant he registered Taeyeon, the shock detonated into pure joy.

“Nuna! You— how are you HERE?”

She looked at him with glimmering eyes, voice going soft and lilting.

“What~ am I not welcome?”

“How could you not be?” His chest was still heaving; the reunion had his heart sprinting for entirely new reasons now. “Nuna, is your schedule finally over?”

She drifted half a step closer, stopping one arm’s length away.

“All done. Didn’t you say you were cutting demos tonight? So I… and Tiffany came to see.”

“How’d the recording go?”

“Mm — finished.” Only then did he register the second figure, and his stomach dropped half an inch before he steadied it into an easy greeting. “Nuna. Long time no see.”

“Seriously — over half a month, right?”

Tiffany sighed a little. That was the artist’s life: schedules ate you whole and a month vanished. She’d only had KakaoTalk to talk music with him — and now, in person, he looked a touch more grown. A touch more handsome, too.

She crossed to the workstation and bent slightly toward the screen, curious.

“Wait — you wrote something new again?”

Ji-ho picked up the monitor headphones and handed the first pair to Tiffany, who stood nearer — then, entirely naturally, lifted the second pair to Taeyeon, his fingertips grazing the back of her hand in passing.

The tips of her ears went quietly pink.

“Two fresh demos, actually. Nunas, give them a listen for me?”

He hit play and angled his body aside to give them the console.

The intro hit like a dare — hard, bright, rhythm out in front — and Tiffany’s eyes went wide and kept going wider, her shoulders already moving without her permission. When the track’s signature title-drop landed, she couldn’t hold in the low “WHOA,” and the look she turned on Ji-ho was open, unguarded astonishment.

“Ji-ho — YOU wrote this? ‘I Got a Boy’? This structure — this genre-splicing — it’s so NEW! And the rhythm is insane!”

She was gesturing with both hands now, fully lit up.

“How old are you again? How is your understanding of music this deep? All those things we talk about on Kakao — I thought you just had fun ideas. I didn’t know the finished builds came out THIS complete!”

At the word Kakao, the smile on Taeyeon’s face stiffened — a flicker, nearly invisible.

A moment ago she’d been floating in the song’s shockwave and the private pride underneath it — see? The person I like is exactly this brilliant — and then Tiffany’s sentence slid in like a fine splinter.

They talk on Kakao all the time? About music? …Only music?

I’ve been buried alive for half a month, barely messaged him — and meanwhile Tiffany…

Her gaze flicked fast between his smiling profile and Tiffany’s shining excitement, and something sour and urgent crept up through her chest, cold at the edges.

He’s this blinding already. Later, when the whole country can see him…

The people circling him will only multiply.

Can this “nuna” really keep her place beside him?
