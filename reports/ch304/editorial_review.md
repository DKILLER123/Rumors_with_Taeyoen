## Continuity and editorial decisions

1. Continue the same **Friday October 4, 2013 hospital night**, after the 22:55 Singapore
   reunion in chapter 303. No new flight, time-zone adjustment or date change is invented.
2. Preserve Song Ji-ho, Im Yoon-a, Seohyun/Seo Ju-hyun, Park Ji-eun, Cheon Song-yi,
   Girls' Generation, Girls & Peace, ECHO, and the established eonni/Fany/Sica forms.
   The Chinese protagonist name and Singapore exonym do not enter the English text.
3. The raw says he never called. Chapter 303 already contains his downstairs call.
   Render the intended distinction: **he did not call ahead to negotiate the journey**;
   by the time he called, he was already at the hospital.
4. Chapter 303 explicitly leaves the thermometer on the cabinet. After he sets her down,
   she retrieves it and resumes the check. It can now fall during the kiss without
   retconning the preceding chapter. He checks/cleans the intact instrument before reuse;
   no clinical reading is invented. Later movement explains its second displacement.
5. The cold and exhaustion predate the collapse. Her sleepless indecision compounds them;
   the chapter does not claim that romantic worry alone caused an infection.
6. Her role was an opportunity and an audition pathway, **not guaranteed casting**.
   She chooses not to pursue it on the new timetable. His offer to speak to Writer Park
   remains an offer; there is no invented phone call, production agreement or new schedule.
7. Keep her “busy until next May” claim in dialogue, the established January comeback,
   October 15 album release (eleven days away), and October 6 concert (two days away).
   Ji-ho's October 5–6 stage commitments and return travel remain unresolved.
8. Her “fever down / leave tomorrow” statement is a hopeful reassurance, not a medically
   certified reading or discharge order. She still has not completed the thermometer check.
9. The porridge complaint precedes Seohyun's return. Treat it as a complaint in advance
   about hospital food; do not pretend she has already eaten the meal still being bought.
   Preserve both the anticipated supper and the delivered porridge/apple as menu contexts.
10. Recast “first concert in Singapore” as this world tour's first Singapore stop, rather
    than introducing an absolute claim about the group's entire concert history.
11. Seohyun connects the masked stranger to a boyfriend in the bathroom. **She does not
    establish that boyfriend's identity.** Her inference and the couple's worry are not
    promoted into confirmed knowledge shared by all members.
12. Preserve the jealous table metaphor, the four-women dilemma, Taeyeon's immediate
    exception, Yoon-a's awkward attempt to classify Fany/Sica, and her refusal to retreat.
    The private pledge is not a public relationship announcement.
13. Preserve his selfish motive for offering the drama and his hopes for her acting career.
    Baeksang/Blue Dragon honors remain aspirations earned through work, not awarded facts.
    The source's “borrowed best actress” sneer becomes the future distinction that never arrives.
14. Keep the kiss, thermometer care, glimpse of pink, wandering-hand protest and mock physics
    in a non-graphic romantic-comedy register. No erotic anatomy expansion or hospital
    wardrobe image. The clothing is continuous from chapter 303, not a new outfit reveal.
15. The Pythagorean/gravity explanation stays conspicuously bogus, spoken by Ji-ho and
    punctured by the narration. It is neither a factual lesson nor endorsed medical advice.
